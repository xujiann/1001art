弗朗索瓦·布歇创作的布面油画，约1750年，现藏于林茨美术馆。

The Light of the World is a 1750 oil-on-canvas painting by the French artist François Boucher, currently housed in the Musée des beaux-arts de Lyon. Commissioned by Madame de Pompadour, the chief mistress of King Louis XV, it marked Boucher's first major foray into religious subject matter.
