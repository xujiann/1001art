卡纳莱托创作的布面油画，约1760年，现藏于达利奇美术馆。

The Bucintoro at the Molo on Ascension Day is a 1760 landscape painting by the Italian artist Canaletto. It features a view of his native Venice with the bucintoro, the ceremonial barge of the Doge.
