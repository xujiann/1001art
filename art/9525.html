Still Life with Kitchen Utensils and Vegetables

让-西梅翁·夏尔丹 (Jean-Siméon Chardin)

1734

法国 / France

属夏尔丹的静物。他画的是厨房里最寻常的东西——铜锅、陶罐、几枚鸡蛋与一条鱼，却以厚而透的笔触把光在粗糙表面上的停留画得无可挑剔。狄德罗惊叹其「魔法」，塞尚与莫兰迪日后都从这里出发。

From Chardin's still lifes. His subjects are the plainest kitchen things—a copper pot, an earthen jar, a few eggs, a fish—yet his thick, translucent touch renders light resting on rough surfaces with unimprovable truth. Diderot marvelled at the "magic"; Cézanne and Morandi would begin here.
