平家物语巻第五より月见図

Moon Viewing, from The Tale of Heike

与谢 芜村 (Yosa Buson)

年代 Date
1700s
媒材 Medium
立轴 / Hanging scroll
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

Drawing from Japanese literature, Yosa Buson copied almost the entire text of the “Moon Viewing” episode from The Tale of the Heike , a story of the rise and fall of the Taira family. The two characters he painted were given nicknames based on 31-syllable waka poems they composed. Seen from behind at the top is Matsuyoi, or “Waiting at Nightfall,” while the person striding below is Mono ka wa Kurando
