Ceremonial paddles are among the best-known examples of Austral Islands art. Although their original purpose is not clear, they were likely used as dance wands. In the early 19th century, many were made as souvenirs for European travelers. It is unclear whether this particular paddle was used locally or made for the market about 200 years ago.
