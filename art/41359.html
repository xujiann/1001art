The Wounded Eurydice

The wood nymph Eurydice was lethally wounded by a snake. Corot borrowed the pose of the wounded Eurydice from a famous ancient Greek sculpture known as the 'Spinario' or 'Thorn-Puller, ' thus she hardly appears at death's door.
