温斯洛·霍默创作的布面油画，约1864年，现藏于克利夫兰艺术博物馆。

The Brierwood Pipe is an oil painting of 1864 by the American artist Winslow Homer. It depicts two men from the 5th New York Volunteer Infantry (Duryee Zouaves). The title may refer to a popular poem of the day about the 5th New York Zouaves, titled "The Brier-Wood Pipe".
