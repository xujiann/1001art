묘지석 [墓地石]

Stone Panel for Royal Tomb

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
late 700s–early 800s
媒材 Medium
石雕 / Stone
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
朝鲜 / Korea

This panel shows a figure with a human body and the features of a rat, which is one of the signs of the Asian zodiac, and is likely one of 12 granite panels from a royal tomb. It is probably from Gyeongju—the capital of the kingdoms of the Silla (57 BCE–668) and Unified Silla (688–935) periods—where large royal tomb mounds can still be found.
