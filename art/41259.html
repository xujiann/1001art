Christ Driving the Money Changers from the Temple

El Greco (Domenikos Theotokopoulos)

年代 Date
c. 1570–75
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
意大利 / Italy

Christ’s expulsion of the money changers who were desecrating the temple in Jerusalem was a popular theme in Italy in this period. To Catholics, the image symbolized the purification of the Church through internal reform and the expulsion of Protestant heretics.
