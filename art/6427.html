温斯洛·霍默创作的布面油画，约1866年，现藏于大都会艺术博物馆。

Prisoners from the Front is an 1866 painting by American artist Winslow Homer. One of Homer's most notable early works, the painting depicts a scene in which Confederate officers surrender to Union Brigadier General Francis Channing Barlow during the American Civil War.
