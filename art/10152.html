Young Woman with a Letter and a Messenger in an Interior (1670) is an oil-on-canvas painting by the Dutch painter Pieter de Hooch. It is part of the collection of the Rijksmuseum, in Amsterdam. This painting was documented by Hofstede de Groot in 1908, who wrote:173. YOUNG LADY IN A VESTIBULE RECEIVING A LETTER. Sm.
