Farming and Herding Buffalo in Summer

狩野秀頼 (Kano Hideyori)

年代 Date
mid- to late 1500s
媒材 Medium
立轴 / Hanging scroll
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

Hideyori is most famous as the painter of a very early pair of genre screen paintings depicting people enjoying autumn foliage at a shrine-temple complex in Mount Takao near Kyoto. In this scene, people are hard at work farming their fields and herding water buffalo in summer.
