This pair of bodhisattvas flanking the larger standing Buddha in this triad came from a temple near the city of Dingzhou in northern China. While the bodhisattvas were not made to accompany this particular Buddha, the three sculptures are about the same age and were carved in a style unique to the Northern Qi dynasty.
