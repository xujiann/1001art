玛丽·卡萨特创作的布面油画，约1880年，现藏于大都会艺术博物馆。

The American artist Mary Cassatt painted The Cup of Tea in Paris ca. 1879–1881. The painting depicts Mary's sister Lydia Cassatt in a typical, upper class-Parisian ritual of afternoon tea.
