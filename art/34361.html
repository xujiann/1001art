Cranes, waves, and pines signify long life and evoke the realm of the immortals, the Daoist paradise Mount Hōrai (Penglai). Cranes also denote happiness and marital fidelity. Shōhaku was celebrated for the eccentricity of both his lifestyle and painting style.
