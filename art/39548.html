This page has survived from an Arabic translation of the Materia Medica , a treatise on the use of the medicinal herbs and plants written in the first century by Dioscurides, a Greek physician living in Asia Minor. The original work was a favorite text of the Abbasid caliphs, the temporal successors of Muhammad; from the eighth century on, they had several copies made for their libraries in Baghdad.
