Bridge of Sighs, Ducal Palace and Custom House, Venice: Canaletto painting

约瑟夫·马洛德·威廉·透纳 (J.M.W. Turner)

年代 Date
1833
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
泰特美术馆 / Tate
地区 Region
英国 / United Kingdom

属透纳的威尼斯题材。1819 年起他三度造访，晚年以水彩速写为本在伦敦作油画：建筑轮廓消融在潟湖的水汽与逆光里，几乎只剩光与色的震颤，是他晚期风格的代表。

From Turner's Venetian subjects. He visited three times from 1819 and worked the oils up in London from watercolour sketches: architecture dissolves into lagoon haze and contre-jour light until little remains but the vibration of colour—the essence of his late manner.
