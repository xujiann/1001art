The embrace of the deity Khorlo Demchog (Sanskrit: Chakrasamvara) and his consort Dorje Phangmo (Sanskrit: Vajravārāhī) symbolizes the union of wisdom and compassion, an important principle of Vajrayana Buddhism. The four-faced, twelve-armed god holds Dorje Phangmo in his arms while trampling on the Hindu deities Bhairava and Kalaratri, who represent ignorance and evil.
