Madame Manet in the Conservatory is an 1879 oil on canvas painting by Édouard Manet of his wife Suzanne. It is held in the National Gallery, in Oslo. It shows its subject in a conservatory, where she often came to help Manet out with sittings for his In the Conservatory.
