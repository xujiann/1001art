Saint Matthew and the Angel is a 1621–1622 oil-on-canvas painting, produced by the Italian Baroque artist Guercino during his early years in Rome and now in the Musei Capitolini in Rome. The work's commissioner is unknown and its first mention in the written record shows it in a 1641 inventory of the Pio collection.
