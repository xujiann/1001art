春日宫曼荼罗図

原文题名Kasuga Shrine Mandala

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
early 1300s
媒材 Medium
立轴 / Hanging scroll
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

A golden path beginning at the bottom of this painting leads up to four red-and-white buildings to the left, then to a fifth building on the right. These are the five shrine halls of Kasuga Taisha in Nara. Above and beyond the buildings is a sacred mountain range––with Mount Mikasa at center, Mount Wakakusa in gold to the left, and the sun or moon rising up behind the mountains. Sacred deer appear here and there. Fiv
