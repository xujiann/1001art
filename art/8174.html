巴托洛梅·埃斯特万·穆里略创作的布面油画，约1675年，现藏于普拉多博物馆。

The Aranjuez Immaculate Conception is a c.1675 oil on canvas painting by Bartolomé Esteban Murillo. It first appears in the written record in 1818, when it was in the royal chapel of San Antonio in the Palacio de Aranjuez in Madrid, after which it is named.
