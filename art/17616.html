Bell (Lai Zhong)

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 800–700 BCE
媒材 Medium

馆藏 Collection

中国 / China

In ancient China, music and ritual had political significance and were linked inseparably to the power of states. During the Zhou dynasty, bronze bells were made in sets of eight to sixty bells. This bell is the second largest from a set of eight. This bell bears an inscription of 118 characters about its owner, Lai, and why it was cast.
