巴托洛梅·埃斯特万·穆里略创作的布面油画，约1668年，现藏于塞维利亚美术博物馆。

Madonna and Child of the Napkin or Our Lady of the Napkin is an oil on canvas painting by Bartolomé Esteban Murillo, created c. 1666, as part of the altarpiece of the church of the Capuchin monastery in Seville and now in the Museum of Fine Arts of Seville.
