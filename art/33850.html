Cherry Viewing Party

宫川长春 (Miyagawa Chōshun)

年代 Date
first half 18th century
媒材 Medium
屏风 / Folding screen
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

This colorful screen illustrates a favorite Japanese seasonal pastime, the viewing of cherry blossoms. Within a curtained enclosure, sumptuously dressed men and women are shown enjoying a puppet play to the accompaniment of music played on the longnecked shamisen .
