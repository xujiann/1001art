The legend of the diving woman of Shido Bay describes a mother’s sacrifice for her child. A woman diver (ama) fell in love and had a child with a disguised aristocrat, Fujiwara no Fuhito, who had come to her seaside village to retrieve a magical jewel from the ocean-dwelling Dragon King.
