Mer à l'Estaque

保罗·塞尚 (Paul Cézanne)

年代 Date
1878
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
朗玛特博物馆 / Museum Langmatt
地区 Region
法国 / France

属塞尚在莱斯塔克所作的风景。这座马赛湾畔的渔村自 1870 年代起吸引他反复前往，红瓦、烟囱与湛蓝海面被他压成平行的色块——毕加索与布拉克后来正是在此写生，直接催生了立体主义。

From Cézanne's landscapes at L'Estaque. The fishing village on the Bay of Marseille drew him back repeatedly from the 1870s; red roofs, chimneys and blue sea flatten into parallel planes of colour—Picasso and Braque later painted here, and Cubism followed directly.
