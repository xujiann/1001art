乔治·斯塔布斯创作的布面油画，约1772年，现藏于格林尼治皇家博物馆。

Portrait of a Large Dog is an oil painting depicting a dingo (Canis lupus dingo) by George Stubbs. It is part of the collection of the National Maritime Museum in Greenwich, London.
