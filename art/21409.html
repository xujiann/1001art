馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
朝鲜 / Korea

The central deity in this Buddhist painting is Sākyamuni Buddha, given that he is displaying the earth-touching hand gesture. He is surrounded by several bodhisattvas and disciples. The inscription below, even though partly erased, tells us that the painting was created in 1846 and was hung in the monks’ residential hall.
