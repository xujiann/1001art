The Bathing Pool (French: Le Bassin de baignade) is an oil on canvas painting executed between 1777 and 1780 by the French painter Hubert Robert. Originally commissioned for the bathing room at the Château de Bagatelle, it is now in the collection of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, in New York.
