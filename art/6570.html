托马斯·庚斯博罗创作的布面油画，约1780年，现藏于皇室收藏。

Portrait of George III is an oil on canvas portrait painting by the English artist Thomas Gainsborough, from 1781. It is held in the Royal Collection. It depicts George III, king of Great Britain. He is portrayed wearing the Windsor uniform and pointing to the Garter star on his chest.
