Nymphéas en fleur

克劳德·莫奈 (Claude Monet)

年代 Date
1915
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
未知收藏 / Unknown collection
地区 Region
法国 / France

属莫奈《睡莲》组画。自 1899 年直至 1926 年辞世，他在吉维尼的水园中画了约 250 幅睡莲。晚年画幅愈来愈大、视点愈来愈低，天空只作倒影出现，画面几近抽象，被视为二十世纪抽象绘画的先声。

From Monet's Water Lilies. From 1899 until his death in 1926 he painted some 250 canvases of the pond in his Giverny water garden. The late works grow vast and lose the horizon—sky appears only as reflection—approaching abstraction and prefiguring twentieth-century painting.
