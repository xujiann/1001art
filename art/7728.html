乔瓦尼·巴蒂斯塔·提埃波罗创作的布面油画，约1752年，现藏于大都会艺术博物馆。

Allegory of the Planets and Continents is a 1752 painting (or detailed sketch) by Italian artist Giovanni Battista Tiepolo. Done in oil on canvas, the allegorical work uses human figures to represent members of the Greco-Roman pantheon, the planets, and four continents.
