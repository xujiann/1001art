卡纳莱托创作的布面油画，约1727年，现藏于阿什莫林博物馆。

A View of Dolo on the Brenta Canal is a c.1729 landscape painting by the Italian artist Canaletto. It presents a depiction of Dolo, a town in Veneto which is now in the Metropolitan City of Venice, on the banks of the Brenta Canal.
