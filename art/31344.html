Friends Visiting in the Mountains

池大雅 (Ike Taiga)

年代 Date
mid 18th century
媒材 Medium
立轴 / Hanging scroll
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

Ike Taiga uses almost the complete pictorial space to create a mountain landscape. The foreground is dominated by several tall trees, which tower over two wanderers at the bottom of the picture, making their way up to the residence in the upper part of the image.
