乔瓦尼·贝利尼创作的木板，约1465年，现藏于Civic Museum of Palazzo Mosca。

The Head of Saint John the Baptist is a tondo painting by the Italian Renaissance master Giovanni Bellini. It is now housed in the Civic Museum of Pesaro. The painting depicts the head of the Saint John the Baptist just after his decapitation, with blood still dripping from the neck.
