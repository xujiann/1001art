Brightwell Church and Village is an 1815 landscape painting by the British artist John Constable. It depicts a view of Brightwell near Ipswich in Suffolk. Although showing his native county, it is some distance away from the area known as Constable Country. It was commissioned by the Reverend Barnwell.
