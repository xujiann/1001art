The Grand Canal seen from the Palazzo Balbi towards the Bridge of Rialto

卡纳莱托 (Canaletto)

年代 Date
1727
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
卡拉拉学院 / Accademia Carrara
地区 Region
意大利 / Italy

属卡纳莱托的威尼斯「景观画」（vedute）。他借助暗箱取景，把大运河与圣马可的建筑透视画得几可丈量，又以明澈的天光统摄全局；这批画主要卖给游历意大利的英国贵族，是十八世纪最成功的「纪念品艺术」。

From Canaletto's Venetian vedute. Aided by the camera obscura, he rendered the Grand Canal and San Marco with almost measurable perspective, unified by a limpid daylight. Painted largely for British aristocrats on the Grand Tour, these are the most successful "souvenir art" of the eighteenth century.
