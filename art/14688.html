Saint Joseph and a Devotee is a 1529 tempera on canvas painting on two panels by Correggio, an attribution first proven in the modern era by Ferdinando Bologna in 1957. The panels are mentioned in the 1680 inventories of the Palazzo del Giardino in Parma as a work by Correggio.
