Rama and Lakshmana slay the demon giant Viradha, from Chapters 2 through 4 of the Aranya Kanda (Book of the Forest) of a Ramayana (Rama’s Journey)

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 1770
媒材 Medium
纸本设色 / Opaque watercolour on paper
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
印度 / India

Viradha was once a celestial musician named Tumburu, who was cursed by Kubera, guardian king and god of wealth, to turn into a demon until he was killed by Rama. During their exile, when the hero Rama, his brother Lakshmana, and wife Sita were venturing in the Dandaka Forest, the demon appeared in front of the divine party and abducted Sita. Rama and Lakshmana slayed the demon and rescued Sita, and Viradha was freed
