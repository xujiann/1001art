Benedetto Portinari's Triptych

年代 Date

馆藏 Collection

地区 Region
佛兰德斯 / Flanders

The Benedetto Portinari Triptych is a group of three 1487 oil on panel paintings by Hans Memling. It is named after its commissioner, who is probably the subject of its right-hand panel, now in the Uffizi in Florence.
