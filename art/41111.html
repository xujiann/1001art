Post Figure (poutokomanawa)

This poutokomanawa may have welcomed guests to a Māori communal house and protected its sacred essence. The Māori conceived of these houses as the physical embodiment of an ancestral spirit: the ridge pole represented the backbone, the side supports represented the ribs.
