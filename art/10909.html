Job Mocked by his Wife is an oil-on-canvas painting by the French artist Georges de La Tour, produced at an unknown date between 1620 and 1650. It depicts a scene from Job 2 in the Old Testament in which Job, a once rich and influential man who in a short space of time lost his children, his possessions and his health …
