Zhang Zihou's Western Inscription

Ichikawa Beian

年代 Date
1836
媒材 Medium
水墨纸本 / Ink on paper
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

This is a rendering of a controversial text by the Chinese philosopher Zhang Zai (Zihou; 1020–1077) in which he envisions an all-encompassing, hierarchical order that links all individuals to the whole universe in a parent/child-like relationship. Ichikawa Beian executed it in a very clean and balanced style. 張子厚西銘 乾稱父坤稱母予茲藐焉乃混然中處故天地之塞，吾其體天地之帥吾其性民吾同胞吾與也大君者吾父母宗子其大臣宗子之家相也尊高年所以長其長慈孤弱，所以幼其幼聖其合德賢其秀也凡天下疲癃殘疾惸獨鰥寡皆吾兄弟之顛連而無告者也
