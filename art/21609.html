Black-Figure Neck-Amphora (Storage Vessel): Departing Warrior with Chariot (A); Battling Warriors (B)

年代 Date
c. 520–510 BCE

克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
古希腊罗马 / Greece & Rome

The two sides of this neck-amphora both depict warriors—one preparing to depart for battle in a four-horse chariot, three doing battle on the other side. Two of those battling hold spears and so-called “Boiotian shields,” with cutout sides, while the vanquished warrior holds only a round shield, apparently having lost his weapon.
