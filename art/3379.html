Self-portrait as Zeuxis Laughing

伦勃朗 (Rembrandt)

年代 Date
1668
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
瓦尔拉夫-里夏茨博物馆 / Wallraf–Richartz Museum
地区 Region
荷兰 / Netherlands

属伦勃朗的自画像系列。他一生画了约八十幅自画像（油画、蚀刻与素描），从意气风发的青年一路画到潦倒衰老——没有哪位画家如此持续地以自己为对象。它们既是技法试验场，也是一部用颜料写成的自传。

From Rembrandt's self-portraits. He made some eighty across oil, etching and drawing, tracking himself from confident youth to ruined old age—no painter has so persistently taken himself as subject. They served as a laboratory of technique and as an autobiography written in paint.
