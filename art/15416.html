The Mermaids (also known as Drowned Maidens; Russian: Русалки) is an 1871 oil-on-canvas by the Russian artist Ivan Kramskoi. It depicts nineteen rusalki, who, according to Slavic mythology, were river or lake spirits who appeared at night in the form of young women.
