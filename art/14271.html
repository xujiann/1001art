彼得罗·佩鲁吉诺创作的布面油画，约1477年，现藏于Pinacoteca comunale。

God the Father with Two Saints is a 1477–78 fresco by Perugino. It was painted for the church of San Francesco in Deruta and shows the town at its base, along with the inscription "DECRETO PUBBLICO DFCTA / ANNO D[OMI]NI MCCCCLXXV[II/III]".
