马远创作的水墨设色，约1190年，现藏于国立故宫博物院。

Walking on a Mountain Path in Spring (Chinese: 山徑春行) is a painting by Chinese artist Ma Yuan (c. 1160-1225). It is painted on an album leaf, which also contains a poem attributed to Emperor Ningzong Of Song inscribed in the upper right corner. The poem reads: 觸袖野花多自舞。避人幽鳥不成啼。
