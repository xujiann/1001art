The Allegory of Virtue is a tempera on canvas painting by Correggio dating to around 1531 and measuring 149 centimetres (59 in) by 88 centimetres (35 in). It and Allegory of Vice were painted as a pair for the studiolo of Isabella d'Este, with Vice probably the second of the two to be completed.
