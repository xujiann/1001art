The Buddhist deity Aizen Myōō channels carnal desire into a lust for spiritual enlightenment. In its principal right arm, this figure once held a vajra , a symbolic weapon used to cut through illusion, while its main left arm still grasps the vajra bell rung to bring one to awareness.
