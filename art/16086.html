尤迪特·莱斯特创作的布面油画，约1630年，现藏于国家艺廊。

Self-portrait by Judith Leyster is a Dutch Golden Age painting in oils now in the collection of the National Gallery of Art in Washington DC. It was offered in 1633 as a masterpiece to the Haarlem Guild of St. Luke.
