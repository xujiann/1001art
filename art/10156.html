Group Portrait of an Unknown Family or Company (1658–1660) is an oil-on-canvas painting by the Dutch Golden Age painter Pieter de Hooch. It is part of the collection of the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna. The painting was documented by Hofstede de Groot in 1908, who wrote:321. FAMILY IN THE COURTYARD OF A HOUSE. De G.
