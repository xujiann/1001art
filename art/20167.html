Sake Pourers with Bamboo and Bellflowers

初代清风与平 (Seifū Yohei I)

年代 Date
c. 1844–57
媒材 Medium
瓷/陶 / Ceramic
馆藏 Collection

地区 Region
日本 / Japan

This pair of sake pourers adorned with with bamboo and bellflowers is attributed to the founder of the Seifū Studio, Seifū Yohei I (1801–1861). Yohei I, the lineage founder, was born to the Yasuda family in the city of Kanazawa, which faces the Sea of Japan.
