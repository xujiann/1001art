The Assassination of Saint Peter Martyr is an oil-and-tempera painting on wood executed ca. 1507 by the Italian Renaissance master Giovanni Bellini. The painting measures 67 by 100 cm and is now in the National Gallery, London. A workshop version of around 1509 is now in the Courtauld Gallery.
