Lochis Madonna

乔瓦尼·贝利尼 (Giovanni Bellini)

年代 Date
1476
媒材 Medium
木板 / On panel
馆藏 Collection
卡拉拉学院 / Accademia Carrara
地区 Region
意大利 / Italy

属贝利尼的圣母子。他一生反复经营这一题材，把拜占庭式的庄严逐步化为可亲的人情，并率先以油彩层层罩染，使肌肤与织物透出温润的光——威尼斯画派的色彩传统由此奠基，提香与乔尔乔涅皆出其门。

From Bellini's Madonna and Child. He returned to the theme throughout his life, softening Byzantine solemnity into human tenderness, and pioneered the layered oil glazes that give flesh and fabric their warm inner light—founding the Venetian colour tradition from which Titian and Giorgione emerged.
