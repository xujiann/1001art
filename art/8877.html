乔治·斯塔布斯创作的布面油画，约1793年，现藏于皇室收藏。

The Prince of Wales's Phaeton is a 1793 oil painting by the British artist George Stubbs. It depicts a phaeton, a fashionable racing carriage belonging to the Prince of Wales and also features the prince's coachman Samuel Thomas in livery with two horses. Also included is the prince's spitz dog Fino.
