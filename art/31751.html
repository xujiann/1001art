Head of a Bodhisattva

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
late 6th century
媒材 Medium
石雕 / Stone
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

This sculpture was originally the head of a standing figure of a bodhisattva—a being who has postponed its own passage to nirvana in order to guide others to salvation. Carved fully in the round, with a serene expression and gracefully curved downcast eyes, it wears an elaborate crown that is secured by knotted ribbons that hang down behind its ears.
