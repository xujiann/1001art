Morning Sun over Heavenly Citadel Peak

丁云鹏 (Ding Yunpeng)

年代 Date
1614
媒材 Medium
立轴 / Hanging scroll
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

A birthday gift, this scroll by Ding Yunpeng from Anhui is among the earliest paintings of Mount Huang (Yellow Mountains), here depicting Heavenly Citadel Peak. The mountains are named after the legendary Yellow emperor who is said to have visited the place to look for the essence of immortality.
