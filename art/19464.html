Coffin of Senbi

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 1918–1859 BCE
媒材 Medium
gessoed and painted cedar
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
埃及·西亚 / Egypt & West Asia

The most common type of coffin during the Middle Kingdom took the shape of a rectangular box with lid. The mummy inside was placed on his left side, facing east, his head behind the two magical eyes. These—in the shape of human eyes, to which have been added the markings of a falcon's head—were supposed to enable him to behold the rising sun, reborn daily.
