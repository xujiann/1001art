卡纳莱托创作的布面油画，约1743年，现藏于波尔迪·佩佐利美术馆。

The Pra della Valle in Padua is an oil-on-canvas painting executed ca. 1741–1746 by the Italian artist Canaletto. It presents an expansive view of the Prato della Valle in Padua.
