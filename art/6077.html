老卢卡斯·克拉纳赫创作的木板油画，约1528年，现藏于乌菲齐美术馆。

Adam and Eve is a pair of paintings by German Renaissance master Lucas Cranach the Elder, dating from 1528, housed in the Uffizi, Florence, Italy. The two biblical ancestors are portrayed, in two different panels, on a dark background, standing on a barely visible ground.
