菲利普·德·尚帕涅创作的布面油画，约1643年，现藏于里尔美术宫。

The Nativity is an oil on canvas painting by Flemish-French Philippe de Champaigne, created c. 1643. It has been in the Palais des Beaux-Arts de Lille since 1804.
