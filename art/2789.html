Haystacks (Effect of Snow and Sun)

克劳德·莫奈 (Claude Monet)

年代 Date
1891
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
大都会艺术博物馆 / Metropolitan Museum of Art
地区 Region
法国 / France

属莫奈《干草堆》组画。1890–91 年他在吉维尼自家田边反复描绘同一堆麦垛，跨越季节与时辰，以证明形体不变而光色万变。1895 年这组画在莫斯科展出时深深震动了年轻的康定斯基。

From Monet's Haystacks series. Painting the same stacks in the fields at Giverny across seasons and hours in 1890–91, he set out to show that while the form holds constant, light and colour never do. Seen in Moscow in 1895, the series famously shook the young Kandinsky.
