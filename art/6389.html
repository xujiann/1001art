Portrait of Angelica Kauffman is a painting by Joshua Reynolds, painted in exchange for a portrait of Reynolds by Angelica Kauffman. It was engraved by Francesco Bartolozzi in 1780. Three autograph copies survive – one was collected by Earl Spencer and sold by him in 1983. It was again sold at Christie's in 1999.
