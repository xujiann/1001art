Athlete Making an Offering

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 450–425 BCE
媒材 Medium
青铜 / Bronze
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
古希腊罗马 / Greece & Rome

Standing in a classic contrapposto pose, with one leg straight and weight-bearing, the other bent and relaxed, this athlete pauses after victory. He turns his head gently, looking down to make an offering with his lost right hand.
