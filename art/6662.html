Ambassadeurs (also known as Aristide Bruant and Aristide Bruant aux Ambassadeurs) is an 1892 lithograph poster by French post-impressionist Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec. The subject of the poster is Toulouse-Lautrec's friend, cabaret singer Aristide Bruant.
