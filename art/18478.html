Ravana addresses Sita in the garden of Lanka, from Chapters 53 and 54 of the Aranya Kanda (Book of the Forest) of a Ramayana (Rama’s Journey)

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 1725
媒材 Medium
纸本设色 / Opaque watercolour on paper
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
印度 / India

The viewer is cut off from Lanka by the surrounding sea and the city’s golden walls in this expansive composition probably intended for courtly display This choice of perspective cleverly emphasizes the captivity of the Sita, faithful wife of the hero Rama. She kneels beneath an ashoka tree, guarded by demonesses. The princess’s abductor, the 10-headed and 20-armed demon lord of Lanka, Ravana, appears twice
