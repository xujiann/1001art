Liu Jun’s paintings are noted for their capacity to create arresting visual narratives that are often imbued with complex symbolic meanings. This riverside scene depicts a pavilion on the left beneath a large tree, surrounded by lotus blossoms floating on the water. A scholar sitting in front of his desk gazes out into the distance at the large, rocky mountains beyond. In the lower right corner, a fishing boat floats
