Nine Scenes from the Tale of Genji

俵屋宗达 (Tawaraya Sōtatsu)

年代 Date
early 17th century
媒材 Medium
屏风 / Folding screen
馆藏 Collection

地区 Region
日本 / Japan

This screen shows nine scenes from consecutive chapters from the Tale of Genji in order from right to left. The far right panel depicts Genji sitting behind a writing desk and gazing out toward a setting sun.
