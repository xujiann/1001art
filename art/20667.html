Enthroned Virgin and Child

Gil de Siloé

年代 Date
c. 1480
媒材 Medium
alabaster with traces of gilding and pol
馆藏 Collection

地区 Region
欧洲 / Europe

The seated Virgin Mary with Christ is one of the most popular themes in Christian art of the Middle Ages. Alabaster was an ideal material to depict the fleshiness of Jesus, the lush folds of Mary’s cloak, and the fine details of the throne and gown. Christ is reaching for something in his mother’s hand that is now unrecognizable because alabaster tends to break or wear away quickly. It may have been a book or a piece
