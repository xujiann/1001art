《小受难》(Small Passion) 系列 37 幅木版画之一。丢勒以密致的木刻线条连续叙述基督受难,是其宗教版画的代表。

One of the thirty-seven woodcuts of the Small Passion, Dürer's densely narrated woodcut cycle of the Passion of Christ.
