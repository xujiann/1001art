This rare thanka was painted in the Tashilumpo monastery in central Tibet for a Newari patron from Nepal. From the seventeenth century on, wealthy Nepalese merchants trading with Tibet often visited the famous Tashilumpo monastery, seat of the Panchen Lamas, and commissioned thankas.
