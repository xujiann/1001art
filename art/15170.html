《圣母往见》是意大利文艺复兴时期画家多米尼哥·基兰达奥的一幅板面蛋彩画，绘制于1491年。现藏于法国巴黎卢浮宫。 这幅画系洛伦佐·托尔纳布尼为佛罗伦萨的巴齐玛利亚玛达肋纳堂订制的。。

The Visitation is a painting by the Italian Renaissance painter Domenico Ghirlandaio, dating from 1491. It now is in the Louvre Museum, Paris, France. The work was commissioned by Lorenzo Tornabuoni for the church later known as Santa Maria Maddalena de' Pazzi.
