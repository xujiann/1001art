Women playing at cross-dressing

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 1680–1700; border added 1800s
媒材 Medium
绘画 / ink heightened with colors on paper
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
印度 / India

In this scene harem women act out the visit of a prince. One of them has donned a man’s royal feathered turban, and a lady affectionately offers a cup of wine as attendants stand by with two more bottles, a fan, and a rose. The master artist of an Indian painting workshop would draw the composition and make visual notations as to which colors and patterns should be used. The drawing would then be given to a colorist
