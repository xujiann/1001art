Persia (Iran)创作的纸本设色，约14th century年，现藏于明尼阿波利斯艺术学院。

This folio depicting two stags comes from a dispersed copy of a medical text titled Manafi al-Hayavan (On the Uses Derived from Animals) ; a Christian doctor who was a court physician to the Abbasid caliph in Baghdad wrote the original in Arabic in a.d. 941.
