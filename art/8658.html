Deposition of Christ is an early 17th-century painting by Jusepe de Ribera It was found on the altar of the Ara Pacis chapel in the co-cathedral of Castellammare di Stabia by count Vincenzo Coppola and donated by him to the bishop of the Diocese of Castellamare di Stabia.
