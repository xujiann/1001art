彼得罗·达·科尔托纳创作的布面油画，约1627年，现藏于卡比托利欧博物馆。

Rape of the Sabines (Italian: Ratto delle Sabine) may refer to either of two oil paintings by the Italian Baroque artist Pietro da Cortona, created c. 1629-1630. One is in the Capitoline Museums, Rome. The other is listed in 19th century catalogues of the art collection at Blenheim Palace, Oxfordshire.
