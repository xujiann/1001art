索福尼斯瓦·安圭索拉 (Sofonisba Anguissola)

年代 Date
1560

馆藏 Collection

索福尼斯瓦·安圭索拉创作的布面油画，约1560年，现藏于爱尔兰国立美术馆。

The Portrait of Prince Alessandro Farnese is a painting by the 16th-century Italian artist Sofonisba Anguissola. It depicts the prince, later the Duke of Parma and Piacenza, as 15-year old boy, dressed in refined courtly clothing.
