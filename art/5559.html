爱德华·马奈创作的布面油画，约1866年，现藏于大都会艺术博物馆。

A Young Lady in 1866 or Lady with a Parrot is an 1866 painting by Édouard Manet, showing his favourite model, Victorine Meurent, wearing a long pink peignoir, holding a small bouquet of violets, and accompanied by an African grey parrot.
