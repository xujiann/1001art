保罗·委罗内塞创作的布面油画，约1570年，现藏于大都会艺术博物馆。

Venus and Mars is an oil painting on canvas painted in the 1570s by the Italian Renaissance artist Paolo Veronese. The painting was commissioned by Rudolf II, Holy Roman Emperor and was one of three mythological and love-themed works commissioned by the artist.
