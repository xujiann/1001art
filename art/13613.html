The Raising of Lazarus is a 1619 oil-on-canvas painting by the Italian Baroque artist Guercino, now in the Louvre Museum in Paris. A preparatory sketch for the work is now in the Metropolitan Museum of Art, In 1682 it was recorded as being in the Naples collection of the brothers Carlo and Francesco Garofali.
