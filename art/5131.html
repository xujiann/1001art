1496

《启示录》(Apocalypse) 系列 15 幅木版画之一。丢勒据《约翰启示录》所作,以震撼的末世意象奠定了他的声誉。

One of the fifteen woodcuts of the Apocalypse, Dürer's celebrated series on the Book of Revelation that established his fame.
