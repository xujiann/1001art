真子飞霜葵花镜

Lobed Mirror with a Tortoise Knob, Musician, and Phoenix

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
late 700s
媒材 Medium
青铜 / Bronze
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

The inscription on this mirror states it was one of a pair made of metals from the south, and placed in a jade box, perhaps alluding to a wedding gift. The meaning of its design is not entirely known, but may refer to the Daoist immortal Hou Jin, who played the qin (stringed instrument), understood the language of birds, and could make predictions based on the reflections on water.
