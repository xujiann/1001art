Madame de Staël as Corinne at Cape Miseno is a painting by the French artist Élisabeth Vigée Le Brun. It depicts Germaine de Staël and it is displayed in the collection of the Musée d'Art et d'Histoire, in Geneva. The work was completed between 1807 and 1809.
