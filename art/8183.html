Saint Justa and Saint Rufina is an oil on canvas painting by Bartolomé Esteban Murillo, created c. 1666, now held in the Museum of Fine Arts of Seville. Saint Justa and Saint Rufina is one of the paintings made to decorate the church of the Capuchin Convent in Seville.
