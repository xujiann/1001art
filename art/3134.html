Farmhouse and Chestnut Trees at Jas de Bouffan

保罗·塞尚 (Paul Cézanne)

年代 Date
1884
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
诺顿西蒙博物馆 / Norton Simon Museum
地区 Region
法国 / France

属塞尚描绘家宅「布丰庄园」的画。这座普罗旺斯的老宅是他父亲的产业，也是他早中期最常画的地方——园中栗树、水池与农舍反复出现，他在此把印象派的外光转向坚实的结构。

From Cézanne's paintings of the Jas de Bouffan. The old Provençal estate was his father's property and his most-painted motif in the early and middle years—its chestnut avenue, pool and farm buildings recur as he turned Impressionist plein-air light toward solid structure.
