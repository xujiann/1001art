Don Diego de Ágreda (1755–1838) moved from Spain to Mexico City in 1784 to assume control of his family’s silver-mining business. Mexican mines no longer used enslaved workers in this period, but the exploitative activities of Ágreda and other Spanish colonists in Mexico caused enduring harm and inequities.
