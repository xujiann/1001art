The Portrait of Dirck van Os is a later painting by Rembrandt (1606–1669), created circa 1658. It is currently in the permanent collection of the Joslyn Art Museum in Omaha, Nebraska. In 1898, the portrait was acquired by a New York art dealer from a private collector in St. Petersburg, Russia.
