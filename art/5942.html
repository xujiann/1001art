Saint Rosalia

安东尼·凡·戴克 (Anthony van Dyck)

年代 Date
1624
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
阿巴特利斯宫 / Palazzo Abatellis
地区 Region
佛兰德斯 / Flanders

Saint Rosalia Crowned by Angels is an oil on canvas painting by the studio of Anthony van Dyck, created c. 1624, one of several works showing the saint produced whilst van Dyck was quarantined in Palermo, Sicily due to a plague.
