청자 상감 연꽃무늬 편호 [靑磁象嵌莲花文扁壶]

Flask with Inlaid Lotus Design

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
1200s–1300s
媒材 Medium
瓷/陶 / Ceramic
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
朝鲜 / Korea

Toward the late 1300s, the overall quality of techniques involved in making celadon works sharply deteriorated. In contrast to translucent greenish blue celadon works of the 1100s, this flask, possibly dated to the late 1300s, has brownish green glaze. And its inlaid design also shows a clear sign of decline in craftsmanship. Nevertheless, less attention to techniques gave way to a freer style pottery called bunc
