Seated Buddha

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 300 CE
媒材 Medium

馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
印度 / India

Multiple images of seated Buddhas and related figures surrounded monuments called stupas that enshrined sacred relics. Made of stucco, which is a lime-based plaster material, the backs of the sculptures were fixed to the platform on which the hemispherical stupa was constructed.
