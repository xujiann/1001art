弗朗索瓦·布歇创作的布面油画，约1751年，现藏于大都会艺术博物馆。

The Toilet of Venus is an oil painting on canvas completed in 1751 by the French Rococo painter François Boucher. It was commissioned by Madame de Pompadour, for her shared retreat with her lover, King Louis XV.
