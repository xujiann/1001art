詹姆斯·麦克尼尔·惠斯勒创作的布面油画，约1863年，现藏于科科伦艺术馆。

Battersea Reach is an 1863 cityscape oil painting by the American artist James MacNeill Whistler. It depicts a view of the bustling traffic on the River Thames as it passes Battersea on the southern bank. After a spell living in Paris, Whistler had settled in London in 1859.
