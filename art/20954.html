Shell with Inlaid Feline

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
100 BCE–700 CE
媒材 Medium
金属工艺 / Metalwork
馆藏 Collection

地区 Region
美洲原住民 / Indigenous Americas

This ornament is made of a spondylus shell, from which the exterior surface has been carved away to reveal a layer of bright orange. The pampas cat inlaid on the surface in multicolored shell and stone has bean-shaped spots on his body, and grasps a gold gourd or fruit.
