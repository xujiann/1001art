Pomegranate Vase

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
1479–1425 BCE
媒材 Medium
blue vitreous faience
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
埃及·西亚 / Egypt & West Asia

This small, round-bottomed flask takes the shape of a pomegranate. The serrated petals or calyxes that trim the top mimic the turned-back outer husk that is characteristic of this fruit. The flask's aperture is small. Not native to Egypt, the pomegranate ( Punica granatum ) was introduced from western Asia or Cyprus in the first half of Dynasty 18, probably between the reigns of Amenhotep I and Tuthmosis III.
