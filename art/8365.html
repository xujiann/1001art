The Death of Hercules (Spanish: La muerte de Hércules, alternatively known as Hercules Seared by the Poisoned Robe) is a 1634 painting by Francisco de Zurbarán, now in the Museo del Prado in Madrid. It belonged to a series of ten paintings on the life of Hercules for the Hall of Realms at the Palacio del Buen Retiro.
