Mont Sainte-Victoire and Château Noir

保罗·塞尚 (Paul Cézanne)

年代 Date
1904
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
普利司通美术馆 / Artizon Museum
地区 Region
法国 / France

属塞尚《圣维克多山》系列。这座普罗旺斯的石灰岩山是他家乡的地标，晚年画了逾三十遍。他以色块的冷暖推远近，不靠透视线而靠色彩建构空间，是现代绘画结构观的起点之一。

From Cézanne's Mont Sainte-Victoire series. The limestone peak near his native Aix occupied him for over thirty canvases in his later years. He builds depth from warm and cool patches rather than perspective lines—one of the starting points of modern pictorial structure.
