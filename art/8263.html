Village Fête (or in Fr. La Fête villageoise) is an oil-on-canvas painting of a village fête by the French Baroque painter Claude Lorrain (real name Claude Gellée), painted in 1639 and given to Louis XIV in 1693 together with its companion Seaport at Sunset, by the landscape architect and gardener André Le Nôtre.
