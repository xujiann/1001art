Visiting a Recluse in Autumn Mountains

Cai Jia

年代 Date
18th century
媒材 Medium
纸本设色 / Ink and colour on paper
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

Underneath the shadows of cloud-encircled mountains, a hermit lives a solitary life amid the trees. This theme has a longstanding tradition in Chinese painting, reflecting the ideal of many educated people to retire into nature for self-cultivation and study. Some retired for religious, particularly Daoist, ideals, that focus on an individual’s harmonious relationship with natural forces. Others retreated in disgust
