馆藏 Collection

中国 / China

The exterior of this jar is ornamented with two rampant five-clawed dragons boldly drawn in rouge de fer . The dragons are in pursuit of the sacred jewel amid cloud scrolls in green and yellow, flanked at the top and bottom with broad registers of cloud scrolls and gadroons.
