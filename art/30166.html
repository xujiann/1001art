Album Leaf of a Scholar in Landscape

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
14th-15th century
媒材 Medium
水墨绢本 / Ink on silk
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

A contemplative scholar sits at ease with his books and writing tools in an outdoor setting. His young assistant prepares the ink as his master looks on perhaps, imagining some future verse. Album leaves such as this typify early Ming taste for Southern Song models and subject matter. The influence of the Southern Sung court was maintained by professional Hangzho artists well in to the Ming period.
