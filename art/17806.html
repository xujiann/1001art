White-Robed Guanyin

张月壶 (Zhang Yuehu)

年代 Date
late 1200s
媒材 Medium
立轴 / Hanging scroll
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

The figure here sits on a rocky elevation representing Potalaka in the Southern Sea, Avalokitesvara’s island home. A vase with a bamboo sprig holds pure water, the nectar of life. Bamboo shines through the figure’s halo, a way of seeing the illusionary world through the Buddhist truth. Avalokitesvara (Guanyin in Chinese), the bodhisattva of compassion, was integrated into the Chan Buddhist canon of venerated
