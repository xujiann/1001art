弗朗索瓦·布歇创作的布面油画，约1750年，现藏于福格艺术博物馆。

Pompadour at Her Toilette is an oil-on-canvas painting by François Boucher from 1750 (with later additions) depicting Madame de Pompadour, the mistress of King Louis XV of France. The format of the painting changed several times after its initial creation.
