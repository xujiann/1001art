The Madonna and Child with Two Angels, Saint Rose and Saint Catherine is an oil painting of c. 1490–1492 in the tondo format by Pietro Perugino and Andrea Aloigi. It shows the Madonna and Child with Saints Rose of Viterbo and Catherine of Alexandria, surrounded by angels.
