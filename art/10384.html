A Sculptor's Model is an oil painting by the Dutch artist Lawrence Alma-Tadema, from 1877. It is held in a private collection. Blending nude art and history painting it depicts a scene from Ancient Rome, a major focus of the artist's work. A naked art model poses for a statue, tying her hair in preparation.
