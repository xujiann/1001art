春雨草堂图

原文题名Spring Rain Thatched Hut

王建章 (Wang Jianzhang)

年代 Date
1650
媒材 Medium
手卷 / Handscroll
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

With its striking simplicity, this painting captures the pure serenity of a private garden in the moist atmosphere of a spring morning on a small river. Painted in the style of Ni Zan, Wang Jianzhang dedicated this work to his patron-friend Gong Weiliu (1611-1680). Gong retired from government service after the fall of the Ming dynasty and constructed a garden-retreat at his native home in Taizhou, Jiangsu province.
