汉斯·梅姆林创作的木板油画，约1465年，现藏于施泰德艺术馆。

Portrait of a Man in a Red Hat is a 1465–1470 oil on oak panel painting by Hans Memling, one of the first portraits he produced in Bruges, with similarities to his Portrait of a Man - its subject is unknown.
