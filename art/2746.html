Rouen Cathedral, Portal, Morning Sun

克劳德·莫奈 (Claude Monet)

年代 Date
1893
媒材 Medium
布面油画 / Oil on canvas
馆藏 Collection
奥赛博物馆 / Musée d'Orsay
地区 Region
法国 / France

属莫奈《鲁昂主教座堂》组画。1892–93 年间，他在教堂对面租屋，架起多块画布同时作业，随光线推移轮换——同一立面在晨雾、正午与暮色下呈现全然不同的色彩。这组画把「主题」从建筑本身移向了光本身。

From Monet's Rouen Cathedral series. Working from rented rooms opposite the façade in 1892–93, he kept several canvases going at once and switched between them as the light moved, so the same stonework appears wholly different at dawn, noon and dusk—shifting the subject from the building to light itself.
