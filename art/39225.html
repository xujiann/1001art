This lavish work depicting a king and his courtiers picnicking in a mountain glade is a definitive example of Safavid manuscript painting from the late 1500s. The painting illustrates a scene from the Khamsa , or Five Poems , by Nizami (c. 1140–1203), one of the greatest poets in Persian literature.
