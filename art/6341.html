Portrait of Mary Monckton is a c.1778 portrait painting by the British artist Joshua Reynolds. It depicts the aristocrat Mary Monckton, the only daughter of Viscount Galway. The painting was commissioned by Monckton herself, and reflects her unconventional demeanour with a mischievous posture.
