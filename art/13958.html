菲利普·德·尚帕涅创作的布面油画，约1663年，现藏于皮卡第博物馆。

Moses and the Tablets of the Law is a 1663 oil on canvas painting by Philippe de Champaigne, now in the Musée de Picardie in Amiens, to which it was given in 1922 by Alexis Marie François de Boutray.
