Bowl with Chinese Landscape

三代清风与平 (Seifū Yohei III)

年代 Date
1893–1914
媒材 Medium
瓷/陶 / Ceramic
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
日本 / Japan

This bowl has a landscape with figures in blue and iron oxide. The scene is set along a river with mountains in the distance, likely in the Jiangnan region of southern China, famous for compositions of the Eight Views of the Xiao and Xiang Rivers and of West Lake in Hangzhou. There are two people in the foreground: one person fishes along the riverbank across from a rustic hermit’s lodge sited on stilts over the wate
