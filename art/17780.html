Gathering at Lanting (Orchid Pavilion)

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
1400s–1500s
媒材 Medium
漆器 / Lacquer
馆藏 Collection
克利夫兰艺术博物馆 / Cleveland Museum of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

This lacquer panel, perhaps part of a piece of furniture, depicts the Gathering at Lanting in 353 CE that took place during the Spring Purification Festival, a day spent traditionally outside in nature. Here, cups of wine are set afloat on a winding stream as part of a poetry competition among the attendants. By the Ming dynasty the painting theme Gathering at Lanting also appeared on lacquer, jade, and porce
