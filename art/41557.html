The Agony in the Garden

Martin Schongauer

年代 Date
c. 1480
媒材 Medium
蚀刻版画 / Etching
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
欧洲 / Europe

After the Last Supper, Christ went to pray on Mount Olivet, bringing along three companions, the Apostles Peter, John and James, who would fall asleep at the foot of the hill as Christ prayed. In the right background of the print, Judas appears with purse in hand, leading the Roman soldiers to arrest Christ. At close inspection, one might note some slips of the burin in this engraving-for instance, in the stray line
