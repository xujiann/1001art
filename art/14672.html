安东尼奥·达·科雷乔创作的木板油画，约1515年，现藏于皇室收藏。

The Holy Family with Saint Jerome is a 68 by 56 cm oil on poplar panel painting by Correggio. It dates to around 1515 and is now displayed in the East Closet of Hampton Court Palace as part of the Royal Collection.
