王概

Wang Gai

1645–1710 · 中国

9 件作品 / works

王概（1645—1710），中国清代画家，浙江秀水（今嘉兴）人，字安节，初名匄，久居江宁（今南京）。曾从龚贤学画，用墨浓重，擅大幅山水与松石。1679 年，他在明人李流芳遗留的画稿基础上增补编成《芥子园画传》初集，专讲山水画法；后与兄王蓍、弟王臬续编二、三集，木版彩色套印，长期是学画者通用的入门范本。

Wang Gai (1645–1710) was a Qing-dynasty painter from Xiushui, Zhejiang, who lived mainly in Nanjing. He studied landscape under Gong Xian and painted in dense ink on a large scale. In 1679 he expanded sketches left by the Ming painter Li Liufang into the first part of the Mustard Seed Garden Manual of Painting; with his brothers he completed two later parts, colour-printed from woodblocks and long used to teach Chinese painting.
