尼古拉斯·马斯

Nicolaes Maes

1634–1693 · 荷兰

46 件作品 / works

尼古拉斯·马斯，伦勃朗最富才华的弟子之一。早年以温情的家庭风俗画著称，尤擅「偷听者」题材与操持家务的妇女形象；晚年转向优雅的肖像画，成为多德雷赫特与阿姆斯特丹的时尚画师。

Nicolaes Maes was one of Rembrandt's most gifted pupils. Known first for tender domestic genre scenes—especially his 'eavesdropper' pictures and women at household tasks—he later turned to elegant portraiture, becoming a fashionable painter in Dordrecht and Amsterdam.
