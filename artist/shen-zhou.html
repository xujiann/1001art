沈周

Shen Zhou

1427–1509 · 中国

55 件作品 / works

沈周，明代吴门画派的开创者、当世文人画领袖，文徵明之师。他一生不仕，以温厚质朴的山水与册页闻名，将文人画的意趣推向新的高度。

Shen Zhou was the founder of the Ming Wu School and the leading literati painter of his day, teacher of Wen Zhengming. Never taking office, he is celebrated for warm, unpretentious landscapes and album leaves that raised literati painting to new heights.
