约瑟夫·马洛德·威廉·透纳

J.M.W. Turner

1775–1851 · 英国

144 件作品 / works

约瑟夫·马洛德·威廉·透纳，英国浪漫主义风景与海景画家，被誉为「光之画家」。他毕生探索光线、色彩与大气的表现，晚年近乎抽象的作品预示了印象派乃至现代绘画的方向。

J.M.W. Turner was an English Romantic painter of landscape and marine subjects, celebrated as 'the painter of light.' His lifelong exploration of light, colour and atmosphere culminated in near-abstract late works that anticipated Impressionism and modern painting.
