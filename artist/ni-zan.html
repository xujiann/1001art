倪瓒

Ni Zan

1306–1374 · 中国

29 件作品 / works

倪瓒，「元四家」之一，文人画理想的化身。他以极简的构图、疏淡的笔墨描绘荒寒的江岸小景，一河两岸、无人萧疏，格调孤高清逸，为后世推为逸品典范。

Ni Zan, one of the 'Four Masters of the Yuan,' embodied the literati ideal. His sparse compositions and cool, dry brush depict desolate riverbanks—water between two shores, empty and still—of extreme refinement and lofty detachment.
