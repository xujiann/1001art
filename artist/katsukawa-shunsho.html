胜川春章

Katsukawa Shunshō

1726–1793 · 日本

31 件作品 / works

胜川春章，浮世绘役者绘的革新者。他一改程式化的演员形象，首创写实的个人相貌肖像；也精于美人画，并是葛饰北斋的老师。

Katsukawa Shunshō reformed the actor print, replacing stylised faces with individualised likenesses. Also accomplished in bijin-ga, he was the teacher of Hokusai.
