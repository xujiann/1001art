石涛

Shitao

1642–1707 · 中国

47 件作品 / works

石涛，明宗室后裔、出家为僧，清初最富独创性的「四僧」之一。他反对泥古摹仿，主张「一画」之说、师法造化与自我，其奔放灵动的山水对近现代中国绘画影响深远。

Shitao, a descendant of the Ming royal house who became a monk, was one of the great individualist 'Four Monks' of the early Qing. Rejecting slavish imitation, he championed 'the single brushstroke' and painting from nature and self; his free, vital landscapes profoundly shaped modern Chinese painting.
