平图里基奥

Pinturicchio

1454–1513 · 意大利

43 件作品 / works

平图里基奥，翁布里亚画派的文艺复兴早期画家，以华美繁丽的装饰性壁画著称。他为梵蒂冈的波吉亚寓所与锡耶纳大教堂的皮科洛米尼图书馆绘制的壁画，色彩绚烂、细节精致，尽显盛期装饰艺术之富丽。

Pinturicchio was an early Renaissance painter of the Umbrian school, renowned for his sumptuous decorative frescoes. His cycles for the Borgia Apartments in the Vatican and the Piccolomini Library in Siena, brilliant in color and rich in detail, epitomize the splendor of Renaissance decoration.
