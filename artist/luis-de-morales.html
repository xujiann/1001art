Luis de Morales

35 件作品 / works

路易斯·德·莫拉莱斯，西班牙文艺复兴晚期最富虔敬气质的画家，因其笔下神圣题材而被誉为「神圣者」（El Divino）。他融合尼德兰的精细与意大利的样式主义，专擅《圣母子》《戴荆冠的基督》等冥想式宗教小幅，情感深沉内敛。

Luis de Morales was the most devout painter of the late Spanish Renaissance, called 'El Divino' for the sacred character of his images. Blending Netherlandish precision with Italian Mannerism, he specialized in intimate, meditative devotional works such as the Virgin and Child and the Ecce Homo, marked by deep restrained emotion.
