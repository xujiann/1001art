河锅晓斋

Kawanabe Kyōsai

1831–1889 · 日本

28 件作品 / works

河锅晓斋，幕末至明治的鬼才画家与讽刺画家。他兼修浮世绘与狩野派，笔力奔放，尤以乌鸦、妖怪和辛辣的时事讽刺闻名，充满旺盛的想象力。

Kawanabe Kyōsai was a fiercely inventive painter and caricaturist of the late Edo and Meiji periods. Trained in both ukiyo-e and the Kanō school, he is famous for his crows, demons and biting satire, brimming with imaginative energy.
