文征明

Wen Zhengming

1470–1559 · 中国

66 件作品 / works

文徵明，吴门画派核心大师、沈周弟子，「明四家」之一。他以精工细致的山水和高洁的品格著称，兼为一代书法大家，享寿九十，影响吴地画坛数十年。

Wen Zhengming was a central master of the Wu School, pupil of Shen Zhou and one of the 'Four Masters of the Ming.' Renowned for refined, meticulous landscapes and upright character, he was also a great calligrapher whose long life shaped the Suzhou art world for decades.
