龚贤

Gong Xian

1619–1689 · 中国

27 件作品 / works

龚贤，「金陵八家」之首。他以层层积染的浓重墨法营造出幽深沉郁的「黑龚」山水，草木葱茏、气象森然，在清初画坛别树一帜。

Gong Xian was the foremost of the 'Eight Masters of Nanjing.' Building dense, brooding 'black' landscapes from layered ink washes, he created a deep, sombre and utterly distinctive vision in early-Qing painting.
