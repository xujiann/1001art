萨尔瓦托·罗萨

Salvator Rosa

1615–1673 · 意大利

49 件作品 / works

萨尔瓦托·罗萨，那不勒斯出身的巴洛克画家、诗人与叛逆的浪漫先驱。他钟情于荒野崎岖的风景、战争场面与巫术奇想，狂放不羁的想象与阴郁氛围，深深影响了后世的浪漫主义。

Salvator Rosa was a Neapolitan Baroque painter and poet, and a rebellious proto-Romantic spirit. Drawn to wild, rugged landscapes, battle scenes, and scenes of witchcraft, his untamed imagination and brooding mood would deeply inspire later Romanticism.
