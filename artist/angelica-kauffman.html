Angelica Kauffman

1741–1807 · 瑞士

46 件作品 / works

安杰莉卡·考夫曼，十八世纪最负盛名的女画家之一，瑞士裔新古典主义大师。她以取材古典神话与历史的叙事画和优雅的肖像享誉欧洲，是伦敦皇家艺术学院仅有的两位女性创始成员之一。

Angelica Kauffman was one of the most celebrated women painters of the eighteenth century and a leading Neoclassical artist of Swiss origin. Renowned across Europe for her history paintings drawn from classical myth and her graceful portraits, she was one of only two women among the founding members of London's Royal Academy.
