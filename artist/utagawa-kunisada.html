歌川国贞

Utagawa Kunisada

1786–1865 · 日本

4 件作品 / works

歌川国贞（后称三代歌川丰国），幕末最多产、商业上最成功的浮世绘师。他一生设计版画数以万计，尤以歌舞伎役者绘与时尚美人画称霸市场，是歌川派鼎盛的代表。

Utagawa Kunisada (later Toyokuni III) was the most prolific and commercially successful ukiyo-e designer of the late Edo period. Producing tens of thousands of prints, he dominated the market with kabuki actor portraits and fashionable beauties, epitomising the Utagawa school at its height.
