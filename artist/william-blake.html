William Blake

1757–1827 · 英国

99 件作品 / works

威廉·布莱克，英国诗人、画家与版画家，浪漫主义的幻想先驱。他自创浮雕蚀刻技法印制「插图诗集」，以圣经与神话为源泉营造独特的幻象世界，生前默默无闻，身后声誉日隆。

William Blake was an English poet, painter and printmaker and a visionary forerunner of Romanticism. Inventing relief etching to print his illuminated books, he conjured a singular imaginative world from biblical and mythic sources—little known in his lifetime, revered ever since.
