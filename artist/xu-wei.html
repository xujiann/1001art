徐渭

Xu Wei

1521–1593 · 中国

33 件作品 / works

徐渭，明代才华横溢却命运坎坷的诗人画家。他开创水墨大写意花卉，以酣畅淋漓、纵横恣肆的泼墨挥洒胸中块垒，深受后世八大、石涛与齐白石推崇。

Xu Wei was a brilliant but tormented Ming poet-painter. Father of the free 'splashed-ink' xieyi flower style, his explosive, spontaneous brushwork poured out his inner turmoil and was revered by later individualists from Bada Shanren to Qi Baishi.
