Pietro Perugino

65 件作品 / works

彼得罗·佩鲁吉诺，是一位意大利文艺复兴时期的画家，活跃于文艺复兴全盛期。其最著名的学生是拉斐尔。

Pietro Perugino was an Italian Renaissance painter of the Umbrian school who developed some of the qualities that found classic expression in the High Renaissance.
