彼得·克拉斯

Pieter Claesz

1597–1660 · 荷兰

38 件作品 / works

彼得·克拉斯，哈勒姆静物画的核心人物。他与同侪共创「单色早餐画」，以银灰的调子、简朴的杯盘与鲱鱼面包，营造出低回内敛的氛围，是荷兰调性静物的奠基者之一。

Pieter Claesz was a central figure of Haarlem still life. A pioneer of the 'monochrome breakfast piece' (ontbijtje), he composed humble arrangements of glassware, herring and bread in restrained silvery tones, helping to found the Dutch tradition of tonal still life.
