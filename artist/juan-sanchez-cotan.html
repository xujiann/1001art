胡安·桑切斯·科坦

Juan Sánchez Cotán

1560–1627 · 西班牙

24 件作品 / works

胡安·桑切斯·科坦，西班牙静物画（bodegón）的开创者之一。他以近乎苦修的极简构图，将瓜果菜蔬悬于幽暗龛中，令强烈的明暗对照赋予寻常之物近乎神圣的静谧，深刻影响了后世西班牙静物传统。

Juan Sánchez Cotán was one of the pioneers of the Spanish still life, or bodegón. In austere, almost ascetic compositions he suspended fruits and vegetables against a dark niche, using stark chiaroscuro to lend humble objects an almost sacred stillness that profoundly shaped the later Spanish still-life tradition.
