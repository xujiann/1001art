让-西梅翁·夏尔丹

Jean-Siméon Chardin

1699–1779 · 法国

101 件作品 / works

让-西梅翁·夏尔丹，十八世纪法国最伟大的静物与风俗画家。他摒弃洛可可的浮华，以质朴的厨房静物和宁静的家庭场景，及对光影质地的微妙把握，深深影响了马奈与莫兰迪。

Jean-Siméon Chardin was the greatest French painter of still life and genre of the eighteenth century. Shunning Rococo frivolity, his humble kitchen still lifes and quiet domestic scenes, with their subtle handling of light and texture, deeply influenced Manet and Morandi.
