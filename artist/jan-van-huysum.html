扬·凡·海瑟姆

Jan van Huysum

1682–1749 · 荷兰

39 件作品 / works

扬·凡·海瑟姆，十八世纪荷兰花卉静物的集大成者。他以浅色背景衬托繁复绚烂的花束，果实与花瓣的质感纤毫毕现，技艺登峰造极，享誉全欧，为荷兰花卉画划下辉煌的句点。

Jan van Huysum was the supreme flower painter of the eighteenth-century Netherlands. Setting sumptuous, elaborate bouquets against light backgrounds and rendering petal and fruit with dazzling finish, his technical brilliance won fame across Europe and crowned the Dutch flower-painting tradition.
