梅因德特·霍贝玛

Meindert Hobbema

1638–1709 · 荷兰

45 件作品 / works

梅因德特·霍贝玛，雅各布·凡·雷斯达尔的弟子、荷兰黄金时代最后一位风景大师。他钟情于水磨坊与林间小径，代表作《米德尔哈尼斯的林荫道》以笔直延伸的透视线享誉画史。

Meindert Hobbema, pupil of Jacob van Ruisdael, was among the last great landscapists of the Dutch Golden Age. Drawn to watermills and wooded lanes, he is celebrated above all for 'The Avenue at Middelharnis,' with its bold, receding line of perspective.
