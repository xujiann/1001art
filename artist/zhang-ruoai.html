张若霭

Zhang Ruoai

1713–1746 · 中国

11 件作品 / works

张若霭（1713—1746），清代画家，字景采，号晴岚，安徽桐城人，大学士张廷玉长子。雍正十一年（1733）中二甲一名进士，入直南书房，以书画供奉内廷。工山水，尤长花鸟，其花鸟得明代王谷祥、周之冕写生一路遗意。奉命鉴定品第内府藏画，参与编纂《秘殿珠林》《石渠宝笈》。乾隆十一年（1746）随帝西巡，途中患病，归京后卒，年三十四，谥文僖。

Zhang Ruoai (1713–1746) came from Tongcheng in Anhui and was the eldest son of the Grand Secretary Zhang Tingyu. He took the jinshi degree in 1733 and served as a scholar-official at court, where the Qianlong emperor valued him for his painting and calligraphy. He painted landscapes as well as birds and flowers, the latter following the descriptive manner of the Ming masters Wang Guxiang and Zhou Zhimian. Charged with appraising and grading works in the imperial collection, he was among the officials who compiled its catalogues, the Midian zhulin and the Shiqu baoji. He fell ill while accompanying the emperor on a tour to the west and died in 1746, in his thirty-fourth year.
