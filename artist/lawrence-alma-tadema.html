劳伦斯·阿尔玛-塔德玛

Lawrence Alma-Tadema

1836–1912 · 英国

82 件作品 / works

劳伦斯·阿尔玛-塔德玛，荷兰裔英国维多利亚学院派画家。他以考据精细、奢华绚丽的古罗马与古希腊生活场景著称，尤以对大理石、金属与织物质感的逼真描绘令人叹服。

Lawrence Alma-Tadema was a Dutch-British Victorian classicist painter. Famous for meticulously researched, luxuriant scenes of Greco-Roman life, he was especially admired for his astonishingly convincing rendering of marble, metal and fabric.
