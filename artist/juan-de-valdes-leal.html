胡安·德·巴尔德斯·莱亚尔

Juan de Valdés Leal

1622–1690 · 西班牙

35 件作品 / works

胡安·德·巴尔德斯·莱亚尔，塞维利亚西班牙巴洛克画派的代表人物。他画风热烈动荡、色彩浓烈，尤以为慈善医院所作的两幅「死亡的寓意」（《转瞬之间》与《世间荣耀的终结》）震撼人心，将虚空派题材推向极致。

Juan de Valdés Leal was a leading figure of the Seville school of Spanish Baroque painting. Working in a fervent, restless style with intense color, he is best known for his two allegories of death for the Hospital de la Caridad, In Ictu Oculi and Finis Gloriae Mundi, which pushed the vanitas theme to its haunting extreme.
