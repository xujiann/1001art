亚森特·里戈

Hyacinthe Rigaud

1659–1743 · 法国

48 件作品 / works

亚森特·里戈，路易十四时代最负盛名的宫廷肖像画家。他一七〇一年所作《路易十四像》，以雍容的仪态、飘扬的加冕披风与炫目的织物，确立了欧洲君主国事肖像的典范，影响绵延一个多世纪。

Hyacinthe Rigaud was the most celebrated court portraitist of the age of Louis XIV. His 1701 State Portrait of Louis XIV, with its majestic bearing, sweeping coronation robes and dazzling fabrics, defined the model of the European royal state portrait for well over a century.
