西蒙·武埃

Simon Vouet

1590–1649 · 法国

40 件作品 / works

西蒙·武埃，将意大利巴洛克引入法国的关键人物。早年久居罗马声名鹊起，归国后成为路易十三时代最具影响力的画家，以明快的色彩与舒展的构图重塑法国画坛，并培育出勒布伦等一代英才。

Simon Vouet was the key figure who brought the Italian Baroque to France. After winning fame during long years in Rome, he returned to become the most influential painter under Louis XIII, reshaping French art with luminous color and expansive compositions and training a generation that included Le Brun.
