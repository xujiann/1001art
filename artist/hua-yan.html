华喦

Hua Yan

1682–1756 · 中国

56 件作品 / works

华喦，清代多才的文人画家，与扬州画派关系密切。他的花鸟、人物与山水皆清新灵动、格调雅逸，尤以生动传神的禽鸟著称。

Hua Yan was a versatile Qing literati painter linked to the Yangzhou circle. His bird-and-flower, figure and landscape paintings are lively and refined, and he is especially admired for his vivid, spirited birds.
