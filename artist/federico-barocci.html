费代里科·巴罗奇

Federico Barocci

1535–1612 · 意大利

43 件作品 / works

费代里科·巴罗奇，出身乌尔比诺，是连接手法主义与巴洛克的关键画家。他以柔和的晕染、温暖细腻的色彩与充满情感的宗教场景著称，虔敬动人的画风对后来的巴洛克绘画影响深远。

Federico Barocci, from Urbino, was a pivotal painter bridging Mannerism and the Baroque. Known for soft sfumato, warm and tender coloring, and emotionally charged religious scenes, his devout and moving style profoundly influenced later Baroque painting.
