菲利普·德·尚帕涅

Philippe de Champaigne

1602–1674 · 法国

50 件作品 / works

菲利普·德·尚帕涅，十七世纪法国巴洛克绘画的重要大师，尤以肃穆内敛的宗教画与肖像著称。他与冉森派的波尔-罗亚尔修道院往来密切，代表作《一六六二年还愿图》以清冷严整的构图与虔敬气质，成为法国古典主义精神的写照。

Philippe de Champaigne was a major master of seventeenth-century French Baroque, celebrated for his austere, restrained religious paintings and portraits. Closely tied to the Jansenist convent of Port-Royal, his Ex-Voto of 1662, with its cool and rigorous composition, embodies the devout spirit of French classicism.
