Alphonse Mucha

1860–1939 · 捷克

79 件作品 / works

阿尔丰斯·慕夏，捷克画家与装饰艺术家，新艺术运动的标志性人物。他为莎拉·伯恩哈特设计的戏剧海报风靡巴黎，以柔美的女性、繁复的花卉纹样与拜占庭式装饰定义了一个时代的美学，晚年倾力于民族史诗《斯拉夫史诗》。

Alphonse Mucha was a Czech painter and decorative artist and the defining figure of Art Nouveau. His theatrical posters for Sarah Bernhardt swept Paris, and his graceful women, intricate floral ornament and Byzantine motifs shaped an era's aesthetic; his last years were devoted to the nationalist Slav Epic.
