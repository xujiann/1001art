椿椿山

Tsubaki Chinzan

1801–1854 · 日本

26 件作品 / works

椿椿山（1801—1854），日本江户后期南画家，出身幕府下级武士（旗本枪组同心之家）。先随金子金陵学沈南苹派花鸟，后入谷文晁门下，十七岁起终生师事渡边华山。擅花鸟与人物写照，设色秀润，肖像兼取西洋明暗法。华山自尽后，他收养并教授其子渡边小华；代表作《渡边华山像》成于1853年（今藏田原市博物馆，日本重要文化财），身后另有木刻本《椿山画谱》辑印行世。

Tsubaki Chinzan (1801–1854) was a nanga painter of late Edo Japan, born in Edo to a low-ranking shogunal retainer family. He first learned Shen Nanpin-style bird-and-flower painting from Kaneko Kinryō, then studied under Tani Bunchō, and from about seventeen followed Watanabe Kazan as his lifelong teacher. Known for delicately colored birds and flowers and for portraits using Western shading, he completed his portrait of Kazan in 1853.
