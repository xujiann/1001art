彼得罗·达·科尔托纳

Pietro da Cortona

1596–1669 · 意大利

43 件作品 / works

彼得罗·达·科尔托纳，罗马盛期巴洛克的领军画家兼建筑师。他擅长气势磅礴、幻觉深远的天顶壁画，巴贝里尼宫的《神意的寓言》即为其宏伟装饰风格的巅峰之作。

Pietro da Cortona was a leading painter and architect of the Roman High Baroque. A master of grand, illusionistic ceiling frescoes, his Allegory of Divine Providence in the Palazzo Barberini stands as the summit of his magnificent decorative style.
