圭尔奇诺

Guercino

1591–1666 · 意大利

67 件作品 / works

圭尔奇诺，博洛尼亚巴洛克绘画的代表人物。早年作品以强烈的明暗对比与充沛的动感震撼人心，罗马路多维西别墅的天顶壁画《曙光》（Aurora）气势恢宏；晚年则转向更为古典宁静的风格。

Guercino was a major figure of Bolognese Baroque painting. His early works astonish with bold chiaroscuro and dynamic energy—epitomized by the sweeping ceiling fresco Aurora in Rome's Casino Ludovisi—while his later manner grew more classical and serene.
