阿尔伯特·比尔施塔特

Albert Bierstadt

1830–1902 · 美国

89 件作品 / works

阿尔伯特·比尔施塔特，德裔美国画家，哈德逊河画派与「发光主义」的代表。他以宏幅而光影璀璨的美国西部风景闻名，将落基山与优胜美地的壮丽气象带入画布。

Albert Bierstadt was a German-American painter associated with the Hudson River School and Luminism. He is celebrated for vast, luminous landscapes of the American West that brought the grandeur of the Rockies and Yosemite to canvas.
