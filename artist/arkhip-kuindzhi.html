阿尔希普·库因吉

Arkhip Kuindzhi

1841–1910 · 俄罗斯

54 件作品 / works

阿尔希普·库因吉，俄国风景画中最富诗意的光影大师。他痴迷于对光的极致表现，《第聂伯河上的月夜》以近乎发光的月色令观者惊叹，将自然景象升华为神秘而崇高的视觉诗篇。

Arkhip Kuindzhi was the most poetic master of light in Russian landscape painting. Obsessed with luminous effects, his Moonlit Night on the Dnieper astonished audiences with its almost glowing moonlight, transforming natural scenery into visions of mystery and the sublime.
