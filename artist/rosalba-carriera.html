罗萨尔巴·卡列拉

Rosalba Carriera

1675–1757 · 意大利

42 件作品 / works

罗萨尔巴·卡列拉，威尼斯洛可可时期的女画家，粉彩肖像画的开创者。她以轻盈柔美、光泽细腻的粉彩笔法风靡全欧宫廷，是当时最负盛名的女性艺术家之一，并当选罗马圣路加学院院士。

Rosalba Carriera was a Venetian Rococo painter and a pioneer of pastel portraiture. Her light, delicate, and luminous handling of pastel enchanted the courts of Europe, making her one of the era's most celebrated women artists and a member of Rome's Accademia di San Luca.
