伊丽莎白·维热·勒布伦

Élisabeth Vigée Le Brun

1755–1842 · 法国

74 件作品 / works

伊丽莎白·维热·勒布伦，十八世纪最成功的女画家之一，玛丽·安托瓦内特的御用肖像画家。她一生创作约六百幅肖像，风格优雅亲切，法国大革命期间流亡欧洲各王室宫廷。

Élisabeth Vigée Le Brun was one of the most successful women painters of the eighteenth century and portraitist to Marie Antoinette. She painted some six hundred portraits of elegant intimacy and, during the Revolution, worked in the courts of Europe in exile.
