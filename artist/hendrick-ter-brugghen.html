亨德里克·特·布吕亨

Hendrick ter Brugghen

1588–1629 · 荷兰

39 件作品 / works

亨德里克·特·布吕亨，乌得勒支卡拉瓦乔派的领军人物。他游学罗马，深受卡拉瓦乔明暗对照法启迪，归国后以强烈的光影描绘乐师、饮者与宗教场景，将意大利画风引入荷兰北部。

Hendrick ter Brugghen was a leading figure of the Utrecht Caravaggisti. Having studied in Rome under Caravaggio's spell, he returned to paint musicians, drinkers and religious scenes in dramatic chiaroscuro, bringing the Italian manner to the northern Netherlands.
