Claude Lorrain

1600–1682 · 法国

105 件作品 / works

克劳德·洛兰，长期旅居罗马的法国巴洛克画家，理想风景画的奠基者。他以诗意的晨昏光线和港湾景致著称，作品成为后世风景画的典范，并直接启发了透纳。

Claude Lorrain was a French Baroque painter who worked in Rome and founded the tradition of the ideal landscape. Celebrated for poetic light and harbour scenes, his work became a model for later landscape painting and directly inspired Turner.
