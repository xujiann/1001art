圭多·雷尼

Guido Reni

1575–1642 · 意大利

72 件作品 / works

圭多·雷尼，博洛尼亚画派的巴洛克大师。他以理想化的优雅、澄澈的色彩与虔敬的宗教题材著称，天顶壁画《曙光》（Aurora）尤为传世，古典之美深受后世推崇。

Guido Reni was a leading Baroque master of the Bolognese school. Celebrated for idealized grace, luminous color, and devout religious subjects, his ceiling fresco Aurora remains among the most admired examples of classical beauty in seventeenth-century painting.
