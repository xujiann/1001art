查士标

Zha Shibiao

1615–1698 · 中国

13 件作品 / works

查士标（1615—1698），中国明末清初画家、书法家，安徽休宁人，字二瞻，号梅壑。明诸生，明亡后弃举子业，绝意仕进，往来于扬州、镇江、金陵等地，晚年寓居扬州。山水取法倪瓒、吴镇、黄公望，兼受董其昌影响，用笔疏简，较同乡渐江更显润泽；书法出自米芾、董其昌，长于行草。与渐江、孙逸、汪之瑞并称"新安四家"（又称"海阳四家"）。

Zha Shibiao (1615–1698) was a Chinese painter and calligrapher of the late Ming and early Qing, born in Xiuning, Anhui. A licentiate under the Ming, he abandoned the examination path after the dynasty fell and took no office under the new one, moving between Yangzhou, Zhenjiang and Nanjing before settling in Yangzhou in his later years. His landscapes follow Ni Zan, Wu Zhen and Huang Gongwang, with Dong Qichang a further influence; his brushwork is sparse and spare, yet wetter than that of his fellow Xin'an painter Hongren. His calligraphy derives from Mi Fu and Dong Qichang, and he excelled at running and cursive script. He is counted among the Four Masters of Xin'an (also called the Four Masters of Haiyang).
