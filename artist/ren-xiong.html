任熊

Ren Xiong

1823–1857 · 中国

26 件作品 / works

任熊，海上画派的早期核心人物。他人物画气势雄强、造型奇崛，尤以一幅顶天立地、直视观者的巨幅《自画像》震撼后世；惜英年早逝。

Ren Xiong was a key early figure of the Shanghai School. His figure paintings are bold and strikingly conceived, above all his arresting life-size Self-Portrait staring out at the viewer; he died young.
