贝尔特·莫里索

Berthe Morisot

1841–1895 · 法国

75 件作品 / works

贝尔特·莫里索，法国印象派的核心成员，参加了几乎全部印象派画展。她以轻盈灵动的笔触描绘女性与家庭生活的私密瞬间，是运动中最重要的女性画家。

Berthe Morisot was a central member of the French Impressionists, exhibiting in nearly all their shows. With light, fluid brushwork she painted intimate moments of women and domestic life, and stands as the movement's foremost woman painter.
