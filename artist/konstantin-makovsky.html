康斯坦丁·马科夫斯基

Konstantin Makovsky

1839–1915 · 俄罗斯

33 件作品 / works

康斯坦丁·马科夫斯基，俄国学院派历史风俗画的名家与广受追捧的肖像画家。他钟情于古俄罗斯波雅贵族的华服盛宴，以《波雅婚宴》一类作品铺陈细腻的织物与珠饰，营造出富丽堂皇的历史想象。

Konstantin Makovsky was a celebrated academic painter of historical genre and a sought-after society portraitist. Enamored of the sumptuous life of old Russian boyars, works such as The Boyar Wedding Feast lavish attention on rich fabrics and jewels, conjuring an opulent vision of the national past.
