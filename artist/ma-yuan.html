马远

Ma Yuan

1160–1225 · 中国

35 件作品 / works

马远，南宋画院大师，人称「马一角」。他开创「一角」式构图，只取景物一隅、余处留白如烟水苍茫，营造出诗意含蓄、空灵深远的山水意境。

Ma Yuan was a master of the Southern Song Painting Academy, nicknamed 'One-Corner Ma.' He pioneered the asymmetric 'one-corner' composition—placing motifs to one side amid misty voids—to create poetic, spare and profoundly atmospheric landscapes.
