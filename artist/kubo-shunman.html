洼俊満

Kubo Shunman

1757–1820 · 日本

7 件作品 / works

窪俊満，日本江户后期浮世绘画师，兼作狂歌，狂歌号南陀伽紫兰。初从胜川春章习画，后受鸟居清长影响，人物取修长挺秀之姿。他以**摺物**（私家订制的小幅版画，多配狂歌）见长，常刻意舍去红色而用灰、褐、蓝的低彩度配色，人称「红嫌」，在浮世绘中别具一格。亦为狂歌本绘制插图并参与编纂。

Kubo Shunman (1757–1820) was an ukiyo-e artist of the late Edo period who was also active as a kyōka poet under the name Nandaka Shiran. He first studied under Katsukawa Shunshō and later absorbed the tall, slender figure style of Torii Kiyonaga. He is best known for surimono — privately commissioned prints, usually paired with kyōka verse — and for a restrained palette of greys, browns and blues that deliberately avoids red, a manner known as beni-girai.
