金农

Jin Nong

1687–1763 · 中国

29 件作品 / works

金农，「扬州八怪」之首。他博学好古，画梅、竹、人物与佛像皆奇古拙朴，独创方硬厚重的「漆书」书体，个性鲜明、别开生面。

Jin Nong was the leading figure of the 'Eight Eccentrics of Yangzhou.' Learned and antiquarian, he painted plum, bamboo, figures and Buddhas with a deliberate archaic awkwardness, and invented the blunt, lacquer-like calligraphy that bears his distinctive stamp.
