青木夙夜

Aoki Shukuya

?–1802 · 日本

77 件作品 / works

青木夙夜，日本江户中后期南画（文人画）画家，名俊明（一作浚明），通称庄右卫门，号春塘、八岳，以山水、人物为主，画风细密。宝历年间经表亲、书家韩天寿引荐，师从池大雅。1776年大雅去世、1784年其妻玉澜去世后，他住进京都东山双林寺境内的大雅堂，自称「大雅堂二世」，守堂作画十余年，并以鉴定、临摹大雅之作见称。晚年移居伊势，1802年（享和二年十月二十三日）殁于松阪。生年不详（欧美馆藏多标作1737年）。

Aoki Shukuya (birth year unknown; died 1802) was a Japanese literati (nanga) painter of the mid-to-late Edo period. His personal name was Shunmei (also written Junmei), his common name Shōemon, and his art names Shuntō and Hachigaku; he painted landscapes and figure subjects in a fine, meticulous manner. Introduced by his cousin, the calligrapher Kan Tenju, he studied under Ike no Taiga in Kyoto from around the Hōreki era. After Taiga died in 1776 and his wife Gyokuran in 1784, Shukuya lived at the Taigadō within the precincts of Sōrin-ji in Kyoto's Higashiyama, called himself its second master, and kept the hall for over a decade, becoming known for authenticating and faithfully copying Taiga's work. He later moved to Ise and died at Matsusaka. (Western collections often give his birth year as 1737, but Japanese reference works record it as unknown.)
