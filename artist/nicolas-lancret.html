尼古拉·朗克雷

Nicolas Lancret

1689–1743 · 法国

44 件作品 / works

尼古拉·朗克雷，洛可可「雅宴」题材的代表画家，华托最出色的追随者之一。他以轻盈明丽的笔调描绘贵族男女的游园、舞会与嬉游场景，气氛欢愉优雅，深受路易十五宫廷的青睐。

Nicolas Lancret was a leading painter of the Rococo fête galante and one of Watteau's most gifted followers. With a light, luminous touch he depicted elegant ladies and gentlemen at garden parties, balls and amusements, their gaiety and grace much prized at the court of Louis XV.
