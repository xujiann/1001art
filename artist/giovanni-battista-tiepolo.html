乔瓦尼·巴蒂斯塔·提埃波罗

Giovanni Battista Tiepolo

1696–1770 · 意大利

118 件作品 / works

乔瓦尼·巴蒂斯塔·提埃波罗，十八世纪威尼斯最伟大的画家，欧洲洛可可壁画的巅峰。他以明亮通透的色彩和轻盈的天顶构图闻名，代表作为德国维尔茨堡官邸的巨幅天顶画。

Giovanni Battista Tiepolo was the greatest Venetian painter of the eighteenth century and the supreme master of European Rococo fresco. Famed for luminous colour and soaring ceiling compositions, his masterpiece is the vast fresco of the Würzburg Residence.
