拉贾·拉维·瓦尔马

Raja Ravi Varma

1848–1906 · 印度

27 件作品 / works

拉贾·拉维·瓦尔马，将欧洲学院派油画技法与印度题材相融合的先驱。他描绘的印度教神话人物经由平版印刷大量复制、深入民间，塑造了印度大众的神祇形象。

Raja Ravi Varma was a pioneer who fused European academic oil technique with Indian subjects. His depictions of Hindu mythological figures, mass-produced as oleograph prints, shaped popular religious iconography across India.
