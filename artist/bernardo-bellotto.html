贝尔纳多·贝洛托

Bernardo Bellotto

1722–1780 · 意大利

67 件作品 / works

贝尔纳多·贝洛托，卡纳莱托的外甥与门生，威尼斯景观画传统的杰出继承者。他游历德累斯顿、维也纳与华沙，以冷冽精确、纤毫毕现的城市全景闻名；其华沙景观日后甚至成为战后重建这座城市的依据。

Bernardo Bellotto, nephew and pupil of Canaletto, was an outstanding heir to the Venetian view-painting tradition. Working across Dresden, Vienna, and Warsaw, he is renowned for cool, exacting cityscapes of meticulous detail—his Warsaw views later guiding the city's postwar reconstruction.
