歌川国芳

Utagawa Kuniyoshi

1798–1861 · 日本

186 件作品 / works

歌川国芳，歌川派巨匠，幕末浮世绘的奇想天才。他以气势磅礴的武者绘（《水浒传》豪杰、历史合战）名震一时，又擅长诙谐的戏画、拟人化的猫与金鱼，想象力天马行空，是浮世绘晚期最富创造力的大师之一。

Utagawa Kuniyoshi was a giant of the Utagawa school and a wildly inventive genius of late ukiyo-e. Renowned for dynamic warrior prints—heroes of the Suikoden and historical battles—he also delighted in caricature and anthropomorphic cats and fish, one of the most imaginative masters of the tradition's last age.
