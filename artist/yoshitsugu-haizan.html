Yoshitsugu Haizan

吉嗣拜山（1846—1915），日本幕末至大正时期的南画家，筑前太宰府（今福冈县）人。少时习汉学，后赴京都从中西耕石学南画。维新后曾任仓敷地方官，1871年因意外失去右手，此后专用左手作画，别号“左手拜山”。擅山水，尤以芦雁图见称，与儿玉果亭、山本梅庄并称地方南画三大家。

Yoshitsugu Haizan (1846–1915) was a Japanese nanga painter from Dazaifu in Chikuzen, present-day Fukuoka. He studied Chinese classics at the Kangien academy in Hita before taking up literati painting under Nakanishi Kōseki in Kyoto. After serving briefly as a local official in Kurashiki, he lost his right hand in an 1871 accident and painted left-handed thereafter, taking the art-name "Left-hand Haizan" (左手拝山). He specialized in landscapes and reed-and-geese subjects, and was ranked alongside Kodama Katei and Yamamoto Baisō as one of the three great provincial nanga masters.
