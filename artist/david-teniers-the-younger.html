小大卫·特尼耶斯

David Teniers the Younger

1610–1690 · 佛兰德斯

51 件作品 / works

小大卫·特尼耶斯，佛兰德斯巴洛克最多产的风俗画家。他以农人宴饮、酒肆嬉闹与卫兵室场景闻名，又曾任奥地利大公利奥波德·威廉的宫廷画师，绘制其画廊藏品的「画中画」，为后世留下珍贵的收藏图录。

David Teniers the Younger was the most prolific genre painter of the Flemish Baroque. Celebrated for his peasant feasts, tavern scenes and guardrooms, he also served as court painter to Archduke Leopold Wilhelm, documenting the archduke's picture gallery in his renowned 'gallery paintings.'
