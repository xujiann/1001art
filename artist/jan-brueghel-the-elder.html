老扬·勃鲁盖尔

Jan Brueghel the Elder

1568–1625 · 佛兰德斯

44 件作品 / works

老扬·勃鲁盖尔，老彼得·勃鲁盖尔之子，因笔触细腻柔润而得「天鹅绒勃鲁盖尔」之名。他精于繁花静物、精微风景与乐园题材，常与鲁本斯合作，共同绘就《感官寓言》等杰作。

Jan Brueghel the Elder, son of Pieter Bruegel the Elder, was nicknamed 'Velvet Brueghel' for his soft, refined touch. A master of flower still lifes, minutely detailed landscapes and paradise scenes, he frequently collaborated with Rubens on works such as the 'Allegory of the Senses.'
