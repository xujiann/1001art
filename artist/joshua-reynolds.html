约书亚·雷诺兹

Joshua Reynolds

1723–1792 · 英国

90 件作品 / works

约书亚·雷诺兹，十八世纪英国最重要的肖像画家，皇家美术学院首任院长。他倡导取法古典大师的「宏伟风格」，其《艺术演讲录》深刻影响了英国的艺术教育。

Joshua Reynolds was the leading English portraitist of the eighteenth century and the first president of the Royal Academy. He championed the classicising 'Grand Manner,' and his Discourses on Art shaped British artistic education.
