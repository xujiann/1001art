尼古拉·德·拉吉利埃

Nicolas de Largillière

1656–1746 · 法国

44 件作品 / works

尼古拉·德·拉吉利埃，十七至十八世纪法国巴洛克肖像画的巨匠。他为巴黎的富裕市民与贵族留下大量气派而生动的肖像，以华丽的衣饰质感、丰盈的色彩与从容姿态，与里戈同为路易时代肖像艺术的双璧。

Nicolas de Largillière was a great master of French Baroque portraiture spanning the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. He left a vast gallery of grand yet lively likenesses of Parisian bourgeoisie and nobility, and with his sumptuous fabrics, rich color and easy poses stood alongside Rigaud atop the portrait art of the age of Louis.
