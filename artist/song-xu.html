宋旭

Song Xu

1525–1606 · 中国

宋旭（1525—1606），明代画家，字初旸（一作初阳），号石门山人，浙江人，后徙居松江。擅山水，兼画人物。上承吴门沈周，论画又推崇李成、关仝、范宽，笔墨雄劲古拙，自成一家。好云游，常寄居寺院，晚年出家为僧，法名祖玄。赵左、宋懋晋皆出其门下，晚明苏松、云间两派导源于他，世称松江画派之源。

Song Xu (1525–1606) was a late Ming Chinese painter. Born in Zhejiang, he settled in Songjiang. He specialised in landscapes and also painted figures, working in the Wu-school tradition of Shen Zhou, while holding the earlier masters Li Cheng, Guan Tong and Fan Kuan to be the supreme landscapists. His brushwork is vigorous and archaic. He wandered widely, lodging in temples, and took Buddhist orders late in life under the name Zuxuan. Zhao Zuo and Song Maojin studied under him, and the late-Ming Susong and Yunjian currents of the Songjiang school trace back to him.
