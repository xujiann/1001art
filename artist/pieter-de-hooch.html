彼得·德·霍赫

Pieter de Hooch

1629–1683 · 荷兰

80 件作品 / works

彼得·德·霍赫，荷兰黄金时代风俗画家，与维米尔同在代尔夫特活动。他以描绘阳光下宁静的庭院与室内场景著称，精妙的空间透视和光线赋予日常生活以秩序之美。

Pieter de Hooch was a Dutch Golden Age genre painter active in Delft alongside Vermeer. Known for serene sunlit courtyards and interiors, his refined perspective and light lend everyday life a quiet, ordered beauty.
