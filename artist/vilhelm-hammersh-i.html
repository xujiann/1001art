维尔赫姆·哈默斯霍伊

Vilhelm Hammershøi

1864–1916 · 丹麦

73 件作品 / works

维尔赫姆·哈默斯霍伊，丹麦象征主义画家，以灰调的静谧室内画闻名。他反复描绘空荡的房间与背身的女子，在极简的色彩与柔和的光线中营造出诗意而内省的沉默。

Vilhelm Hammershøi was a Danish Symbolist painter known for muted grey interiors. Repeatedly painting empty rooms and women seen from behind, he created a poetic, introspective silence out of spare colour and soft light.
