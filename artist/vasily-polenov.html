瓦西里·波列诺夫

Vasily Polenov

1844–1927 · 俄罗斯

55 件作品 / works

瓦西里·波列诺夫，俄国「巡回展览画派」的代表画家与杰出美术教育家。他兼擅风景与历史题材，《莫斯科小院》以质朴亲切的光色捕捉市井午后，开创了俄国「情绪风景」的一脉，并培育了列维坦等一代名家。

Vasily Polenov was a leading painter of the Russian Peredvizhniki (Wanderers) and an influential teacher. Equally gifted in landscape and history painting, his intimate Moscow Courtyard, bathed in gentle light, helped found the Russian tradition of the lyrical 'mood landscape' and shaped pupils such as Levitan.
