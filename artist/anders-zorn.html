Anders Zorn

安德斯·佐恩，瑞典画家、蚀刻版画家与雕塑家，十九世纪末最国际化的瑞典艺术家。他是出色的肖像与裸体画家，善以极简的「佐恩调色板」捕捉水光与肌肤，也深情描绘家乡达拉纳的民俗。

Anders Zorn was a Swedish painter, etcher and sculptor, and the most international Swedish artist of the late nineteenth century. A brilliant painter of portraits and nudes, he captured water and flesh with the spare 'Zorn palette' and lovingly recorded the folk life of his native Dalarna.
