伊萨克·列维坦

Isaac Levitan

1860–1900 · 俄罗斯

64 件作品 / works

伊萨克·列维坦，俄罗斯巡回展览画派风景大师，「心境风景」的代表。他与作家契诃夫交谊深厚，以抒情而质朴的笔调描绘俄罗斯大地的辽阔与忧郁，深刻影响了俄国风景画。

Isaac Levitan was a master of the Russian Peredvizhniki landscape and of the lyrical 'mood landscape.' A close friend of the writer Chekhov, he painted the vastness and melancholy of the Russian land with poetic simplicity, profoundly shaping Russian landscape art.
