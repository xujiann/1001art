Willem van de Velde the Younger

1633–1707 · 荷兰

48 件作品 / works

小威廉·凡·德·维尔德，荷兰黄金时代最杰出的海景画家。他以精准而富诗意的笔触描绘舰船、海战与波光云影，后赴英国供职于查理二世宫廷，其海洋画风影响英国画坛逾一个世纪。

Willem van de Velde the Younger was the foremost marine painter of the Dutch Golden Age. Rendering ships, sea battles and the play of light on water with precision and poetry, he moved to England to serve Charles II, shaping British marine painting for over a century.
