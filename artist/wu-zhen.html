吴镇

Wu Zhen

1280–1354 · 中国

21 件作品 / works

吴镇，「元四家」之一，隐居不仕的高士。他擅画墨竹与渔父山水，用墨湿润苍茫、书法气息浓厚，意境冷寂而深远。

Wu Zhen, one of the 'Four Masters of the Yuan,' was a recluse who never served. Painting ink bamboo and misty fishermen-in-landscape with a wet, calligraphic brush, he achieved a still, remote and profound mood.
