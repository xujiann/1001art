王翚

Wang Hui

1632–1717 · 中国

50 件作品 / works

王翚，清初「四王」之一、虞山派领袖。他集宋元诸家山水之大成，笔墨精严，曾奉命主持绘制《康熙南巡图》长卷，是清代正统画派的集大成者。

Wang Hui was a leader of the orthodox 'Four Wangs' school of the early Qing. Synthesising the great Song and Yuan landscape traditions into meticulous brushwork, he directed the monumental scrolls of the Kangxi Emperor's Southern Inspection Tour.
