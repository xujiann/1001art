铃木春信

Suzuki Harunobu

1724–1770 · 日本

98 件作品 / works

铃木春信，约 1765 年多色套印「锦绘」的开创者，使浮世绘进入full-color 时代。他以纤秀诗意的画风描绘年轻恋人与市井雅趣，色调柔美、意境含蓄。

Suzuki Harunobu pioneered the full-colour nishiki-e ('brocade print') around 1765, ushering ukiyo-e into the era of colour. His delicate, poetic images of youthful lovers and everyday elegance are prized for their soft palette and lyrical restraint.
