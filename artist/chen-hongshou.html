陈洪绶

Chen Hongshou

1598–1652 · 中国

52 件作品 / works

陈洪绶，明末清初人物画大师。他的人物造型高古夸张、衣纹如铁线盘曲，别具奇趣；所作版画插图（如《水浒叶子》）流传极广，影响深远。

Chen Hongshou was a master of figure painting of the late Ming and early Qing. His figures are archaistic, elongated and strangely compelling; his widely circulated woodblock illustrations were hugely influential.
