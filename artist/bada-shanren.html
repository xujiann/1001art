八大山人

Bada Shanren

1626–1705 · 中国

48 件作品 / works

八大山人，明朝宗室，国破后削发为僧。他以极简而奇崛的水墨描绘鱼、鸟与山水，形象孤傲、意味冷峭，饱含亡国之痛与反讽，是个性派绘画的开山灵魂。

Bada Shanren was a Ming prince who became a Chan monk after the dynasty fell. His radically spare, eccentric ink paintings of fish, birds and landscapes—aloof, sardonic and charged with the grief of loss—make him a founding spirit of individualist painting.
