威廉·卡尔夫

Willem Kalf

1619–1693 · 荷兰

38 件作品 / works

威廉·卡尔夫，荷兰「炫饰静物」的巅峰代表。他偏爱描绘中国瓷器、鹦鹉螺杯与银器等奢华器物，在幽暗背景中令光泽与色彩熠熠生辉，将静物画提升至近乎肖像的庄重气度。

Willem Kalf was the supreme master of the Dutch pronkstilleven, or sumptuous still life. Favoring luxurious objects—Chinese porcelain, nautilus cups and silver—he made their surfaces glow with light and color against dark grounds, lending still life an almost portrait-like gravity.
