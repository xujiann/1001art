让-巴蒂斯特·格勒兹

Jean-Baptiste Greuze

1725–1805 · 法国

55 件作品 / works

让-巴蒂斯特·格勒兹，十八世纪法国感伤风俗画的代表人物。他以《乡村订婚》一类富于道德训诫的家庭场景打动观众，为狄德罗所激赏，其煽情叙事与朴素平民题材，回应了洛可可之外对真挚情感的追求。

Jean-Baptiste Greuze was the leading figure of sentimental genre painting in eighteenth-century France. With moralizing domestic scenes such as The Village Betrothal, admired by Diderot, his emotive storytelling and humble subjects answered a growing appetite for sincerity beyond Rococo elegance.
