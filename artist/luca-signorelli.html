卢卡·西尼奥雷利

Luca Signorelli

1450–1523 · 意大利

47 件作品 / works

卢卡·西尼奥雷利，意大利文艺复兴早期的重要画家。他在奥尔维耶托大教堂绘制的《末日审判》系列壁画气势撼人，以对人体解剖与肌肉动感的大胆刻画著称，直接启发了米开朗琪罗。

Luca Signorelli was an important painter of the early Italian Renaissance. His powerful Last Judgment fresco cycle in Orvieto Cathedral, celebrated for its bold rendering of the muscular nude in dramatic movement, directly influenced Michelangelo.
