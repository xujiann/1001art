阿尔伯特·库普

Aelbert Cuyp

1620–1691 · 荷兰

68 件作品 / works

阿尔伯特·库普，多德雷赫特的风景大师。他将意大利式的金色暖光引入荷兰田园，河畔牛群沐浴在夕照之中，静谧而辉煌，其光影处理深受后世英国风景画家推崇。

Aelbert Cuyp was the great landscapist of Dordrecht. He bathed the Dutch countryside in a golden, Italianate light—cattle resting by luminous rivers in the glow of evening—an atmospheric treatment later much admired by British landscape painters.
