月冈芳年

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi

1839–1892 · 日本

24 件作品 / works

月冈芳年，浮世绘最后的大师，跨越幕末与明治维新。他以《月百姿》《新形三十六怪撰》等系列著称，将历史、鬼怪与人物心理刻画得淋漓尽致，为衰落中的浮世绘注入最后的辉煌。

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi was the last great master of ukiyo-e, bridging the end of the Edo era and modern Meiji Japan. Famed for series such as One Hundred Aspects of the Moon, he brought intense psychological and dramatic power to historical and ghostly subjects, a final flowering of the tradition.
