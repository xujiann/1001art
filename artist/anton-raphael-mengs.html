安东·拉斐尔·门斯

Anton Raphael Mengs

1728–1779 · 德国

38 件作品 / works

安东·拉斐尔·门斯，德意志-波希米亚裔画家，十八世纪新古典主义的奠基者之一。他与温克尔曼交往密切，以罗马阿尔巴尼别墅的天顶壁画《帕纳索斯山》确立典范，其理论与实践共同推动了对古典理想的回归。

Anton Raphael Mengs was a German-Bohemian painter and a founding figure of eighteenth-century Neoclassicism. A close associate of Winckelmann, he set a model with his ceiling fresco Parnassus at the Villa Albani in Rome, and through both theory and practice helped drive the return to classical ideals.
