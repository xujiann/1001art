郑燮

Zheng Xie

1693–1766 · 中国

19 件作品 / works

郑燮（郑板桥），「扬州八怪」中最受喜爱者。他画兰、竹、石清劲潇洒，配以自创的「六分半书」，诗书画三绝；为官清廉爱民，是一位耿介的文人。

Zheng Xie (Zheng Banqiao) was the best-loved of the 'Eight Eccentrics of Yangzhou.' His crisp ink bamboo, orchids and rocks, paired with his self-invented 'six-and-a-half-part' calligraphy, unite poetry, calligraphy and painting; he was also an upright, people-loving magistrate.
