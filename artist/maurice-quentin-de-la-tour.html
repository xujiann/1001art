莫里斯·康坦·德·拉图尔

Maurice Quentin de La Tour

1704–1788 · 法国

46 件作品 / works

莫里斯·康坦·德·拉图尔，十八世纪法国最伟大的粉彩肖像画家。他以粉彩捕捉人物神情的细腻与鲜活著称，人称「灵魂的绘写者」，曾为路易十五、蓬帕杜夫人及众多启蒙名士传神写照。

Maurice Quentin de La Tour was the greatest French pastel portraitist of the eighteenth century. Renowned for capturing the vivacity and subtle expression of his sitters, and dubbed the painter of souls, he portrayed Louis XV, Madame de Pompadour and many luminaries of the Enlightenment.
