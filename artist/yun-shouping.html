恽寿平

Yun Shouping

1633–1690 · 中国

40 件作品 / works

恽寿平，清初「六大家」之一，复兴了「没骨」花卉画法。他不用墨线勾勒，直接以色彩晕染花卉，清丽透明、生趣盎然，别开生面。

Yun Shouping, one of the 'Six Masters of the early Qing,' revived the 'boneless' (mogu) technique of flower painting. Dispensing with ink outlines and modelling blossoms directly in colour, he created delicate, luminous and lifelike studies.
