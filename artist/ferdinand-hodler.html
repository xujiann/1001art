费迪南德·霍德勒

Ferdinand Hodler

1853–1918 · 瑞士

71 件作品 / works

费迪南德·霍德勒，瑞士象征主义画家，提出以对称与重复构图的「平行主义」。他创作纪念碑式的人物、寓言与阿尔卑斯风景，是十九世纪末最重要的瑞士艺术家。

Ferdinand Hodler was a Swiss Symbolist painter who developed 'Parallelism,' a principle of symmetry and rhythmic repetition. His monumental figures, allegories and Alpine landscapes make him the foremost Swiss artist of the late nineteenth century.
