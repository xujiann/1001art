雅各布·约尔丹斯

Jacob Jordaens

1593–1678 · 佛兰德斯

71 件作品 / works

雅各布·约尔丹斯，佛兰德斯巴洛克三巨头之一（与鲁本斯、凡·戴克并列）。他以充满生活气息的风俗与神话场景著称，人物丰腴、色彩浓烈，尤擅描绘热闹的民间盛宴。

Jacob Jordaens was one of the three great Flemish Baroque painters, alongside Rubens and van Dyck. He is known for earthy genre and mythological scenes with full-bodied figures and rich colour, especially his boisterous depictions of popular feasts.
