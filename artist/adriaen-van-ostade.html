阿德里安·凡·奥斯塔德

Adriaen van Ostade

1610–1685 · 荷兰

54 件作品 / works

阿德里安·凡·奥斯塔德，哈勒姆农民风俗画的代表大师。他以温厚幽默的笔调描绘农舍与酒肆中的市井生活，光影柔和、人物生动，与其师弗兰斯·哈尔斯及布劳威尔的传统一脉相承。

Adriaen van Ostade was the leading painter of peasant genre in Haarlem. With warm humor he depicted cottage interiors and tavern life, his figures animated by soft, glowing light, carrying forward the tradition of his teacher Frans Hals and of Adriaen Brouwer.
