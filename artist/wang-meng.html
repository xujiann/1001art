王蒙

Wang Meng

1308–1385 · 中国

19 件作品 / works

王蒙，「元四家」之一，赵孟頫之外孙。他的山水繁密苍郁、皴法层叠繁复，以「解索皴」「牛毛皴」写出重峦叠嶂的蓬勃生机，在四家中最见繁复之美。

Wang Meng, one of the 'Four Masters of the Yuan' and grandson of Zhao Mengfu, painted densely textured, restless mountain landscapes. His layered, intricate brushwork conjures teeming, luxuriant peaks—the most complex vision among the four.
