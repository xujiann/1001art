Hendrick Goltzius

1558–1617 · 荷兰

2 件作品 / works

亨德里克·霍尔奇乌斯，北方风格主义的代表人物、荷兰最伟大的雕版大师。他以出神入化的刻刀技艺创造出富于韵律的「膨胀线条」，晚年方转攻油画，是版画艺术承前启后的关键人物。

Hendrick Goltzius was a leading figure of Northern Mannerism and the greatest Dutch engraver of his age. His virtuoso handling of the burin produced a rhythmic swelling line of extraordinary control, and only late in life did he turn to painting.
