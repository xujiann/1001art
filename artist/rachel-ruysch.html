拉赫尔·勒伊斯

Rachel Ruysch

1664–1750 · 荷兰

38 件作品 / works

拉赫尔·勒伊斯，荷兰最负盛名的女性花卉画家。她以深色背景衬托精巧的花果昆虫构图，笔触细腻、植物学般精确，艺术生涯逾六十载，是十八世纪最受推崇的静物名家之一。

Rachel Ruysch was the most renowned woman flower painter of the Netherlands. Composing intricate arrangements of blossoms, fruit and insects against dark grounds with delicate, near-botanical precision, she sustained a career of over sixty years as one of the most admired still-life painters of her time.
