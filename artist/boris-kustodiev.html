鲍里斯·库斯托季耶夫

Boris Kustodiev

1878–1927 · 俄罗斯

53 件作品 / works

鲍里斯·库斯托季耶夫，二十世纪初俄国民俗风情的欢快歌者。他以明艳饱满的色彩描绘外省市镇的集市、狂欢节与丰腴的商人之妻，《品茶的商妇》等作品将旧俄生活定格为一幅幅热烈而怀旧的画卷。

Boris Kustodiev was the joyful chronicler of Russian folk life in the early twentieth century. In vivid, saturated color he painted provincial fairs, Shrovetide festivals, and buxom merchants' wives, and works such as The Merchant's Wife at Tea preserve old Russia in scenes of warm, nostalgic exuberance.
