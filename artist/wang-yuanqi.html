王原祁

Wang Yuanqi

1642–1715 · 中国

43 件作品 / works

王原祁，王时敏之孙、清初「四王」之一，画论最富理论性者。他以董其昌的构成理念营造层峦叠嶂的纪念碑式山水，主持康熙朝宫廷画事，影响清代画坛甚巨。

Wang Yuanqi, grandson of Wang Shimin and one of the 'Four Wangs,' was the most theoretical of the group. Building monumental landscapes from Dong Qichang's structural ideas, he served as the Kangxi court's painting authority and shaped Qing painting deeply.
