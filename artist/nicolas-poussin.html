尼古拉·普桑

Nicolas Poussin

1594–1665 · 法国

104 件作品 / works

尼古拉·普桑，法国古典主义绘画的奠基者，一生大半在罗马度过。他取材神话与历史，以理性的构图、清晰的形体与庄重的秩序，为法国学院派艺术树立了典范。

Nicolas Poussin was the founder of French classical painting, who spent most of his life in Rome. Drawing on myth and history, his rational compositions, clear forms and grave order set the standard for the French academic tradition.
