让-艾蒂安·利奥塔尔

Jean-Étienne Liotard

1702–1789 · 瑞士

50 件作品 / works

让-艾蒂安·利奥塔尔，瑞士裔肖像画家，十八世纪最杰出的粉彩大师。他以细腻入微的写实和柔润的粉彩笔触闻名，代表作《美丽的巧克力女孩》尤为脍炙人口；旅居君士坦丁堡的经历也令他常着东方装束作画，得名「土耳其画家」。

Jean-Étienne Liotard was a Swiss portraitist and the finest pastel master of the eighteenth century. Celebrated for his meticulous realism and the soft luminosity of his pastels—epitomized by The Chocolate Girl—his years in Constantinople led him to paint in Turkish dress, earning him the nickname 'the Turkish painter.'
