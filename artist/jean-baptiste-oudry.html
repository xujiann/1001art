让-巴蒂斯特·乌德里

Jean-Baptiste Oudry

1686–1755 · 法国

45 件作品 / works

让-巴蒂斯特·乌德里，法国洛可可时期杰出的动物、狩猎与静物画家。他以精准而生动的笔触描绘走兽、猎犬与野味，深得王室喜爱，并长期主持博韦挂毯厂，为拉封丹《寓言》所作插图亦广为流传。

Jean-Baptiste Oudry was an outstanding Rococo painter of animals, the hunt and still life. His precise, vivid renderings of beasts, hounds and game won royal favor, and he long directed the Beauvais tapestry works, while his illustrations for La Fontaine's Fables enjoyed wide renown.
