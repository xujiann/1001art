阿德里安·布劳威尔

Adriaen Brouwer

1605–1638 · 佛兰德斯

40 件作品 / works

阿德里安·布劳威尔，横跨佛兰德斯与荷兰的农民生活画家。他以粗犷生动的笔触描绘酒肆斗殴、烟客与市井百态，情感炽烈而直率，虽英年早逝，却深刻影响了特尼耶斯与奥斯塔德。

Adriaen Brouwer painted low-life scenes across both Flanders and Holland. With rough, vivid brushwork he captured tavern brawls, smokers and rustic revelry with raw immediacy, and though he died young, he profoundly influenced Teniers and Ostade.
