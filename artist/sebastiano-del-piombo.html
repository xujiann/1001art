塞巴斯蒂亚诺·德尔·皮翁博

Sebastiano del Piombo

1485–1547 · 意大利

44 件作品 / works

塞巴斯蒂亚诺·德尔·皮翁博，威尼斯出身、后活跃于罗马的盛期文艺复兴画家。他将威尼斯的丰润色彩与罗马的宏伟造型熔于一炉，与米开朗琪罗交谊深厚，《拉撒路的复活》即为二人合作精神的杰作。

Sebastiano del Piombo was a High Renaissance painter, Venetian by birth but active in Rome. Fusing Venetian richness of color with Roman monumentality, he was a close associate of Michelangelo, and his Raising of Lazarus embodies the fruits of that collaboration.
