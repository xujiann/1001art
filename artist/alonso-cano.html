阿隆索·卡诺

Alonso Cano

1601–1667 · 西班牙

39 件作品 / works

阿隆索·卡诺，西班牙巴洛克时期集绘画、雕塑与建筑于一身的全才，素有「西班牙的米开朗琪罗」之称。他画风典雅细腻、造型温润，晚年主持格拉纳达主教座堂正立面的设计，为西班牙巴洛克建筑留下典范。

Alonso Cano was a Spanish Baroque master who excelled equally as painter, sculptor, and architect, earning the epithet 'the Spanish Michelangelo.' His refined, tender style is matched by his design for the façade of Granada Cathedral, a landmark of Spanish Baroque architecture.
