北尾政美

Kitao Masayoshi

1764–1824 · 日本

1 件作品 / works

北尾政美，日本江户后期浮世绘师，师从北尾重政，早年为黄表纸等通俗读物作插图。宽政六年（1794）受聘为津山藩御用绘师并剃发，后改号锹形蕙斋，又随幕府奥绘师狩野惟信习狩野派笔法。自宽政七年（1795）起刊行《略画式》《鸟兽略画式》等画谱，以极简笔线概括人物鸟兽形体；文化六年（1809）作鸟瞰名所图《江户一目图屏风》（津山乡土博物馆藏）。

Kitao Masayoshi trained under Kitao Shigemasa and began as an illustrator of popular fiction in Edo. In 1794 he was engaged as official painter to the Tsuyama domain and took the tonsure, later adopting the name Kuwagata Keisai; he also studied Kanō-school painting under the shogunal painter Kanō Korenobu. From 1795 he issued the Ryakugashiki drawing manuals, reducing figures, birds and animals to a few strokes, and in 1809 painted the panoramic bird's-eye view of the city, Edo ichimoku-zu byōbu.
