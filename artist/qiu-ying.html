仇英

Qiu Ying

1494–1548 · 中国

50 件作品 / works

仇英，出身寒微而跻身「明四家」的职业画家巨匠。他工笔精丽，尤擅青绿山水与人物故事画，摹古而能出新，设色富丽、一丝不苟。

Qiu Ying, of humble origins, rose to be one of the 'Four Masters of the Ming' and a supreme professional painter. A master of meticulous blue-green landscapes and narrative figure scenes, he copied the ancients yet renewed them with rich, exacting colour.
