歌川丰国

Utagawa Toyokuni

1769–1825 · 日本

23 件作品 / works

歌川丰国，浮世绘最强画派——歌川派的奠基者。他以富于动感、贴近舞台的役者绘风靡一时，门下国芳、国贞等名家辈出，主导了幕末浮世绘。

Utagawa Toyokuni founded the Utagawa school, the most powerful ukiyo-e lineage. Celebrated for dynamic, stage-vivid actor prints, he trained masters such as Kuniyoshi and Kunisada and dominated late-Edo ukiyo-e.
