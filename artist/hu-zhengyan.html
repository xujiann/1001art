胡正言

Hu Zhengyan

1582–1672 · 中国

14 件作品 / works

胡正言，明末清初书画篆刻家、出版家，安徽休宁人，长期寓居南京鸡笼山下，因庭中植竹十余竿而名其居为十竹斋。他主持刻成《十竹斋书画谱》（1619—1633 年间分谱陆续刊成）与《十竹斋笺谱》（1644）：前者以饾版分色分版叠印，使木版套印能再现毛笔的浓淡晕染；后者兼用拱花压出无墨凸纹。二书并为中国彩色版画的代表成果。

Hu Zhengyan was a seal carver, calligrapher and publisher of the late Ming and early Qing, born in Xiuning, Anhui, and long resident in Nanjing. His Ten Bamboo Studio issued the Manual of Calligraphy and Painting, produced in instalments between 1619 and 1633, and the Album of Letter Paper of 1644. The first used douban multi-block colour printing to reproduce the tonal washes of brushwork; the second added gonghua blind embossing for uninked relief designs.
