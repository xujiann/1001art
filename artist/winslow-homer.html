温斯洛·霍默

Winslow Homer

1836–1910 · 美国

136 件作品 / works

温斯洛·霍默，美国现实主义画家，最初以南北战争的插画起家。他以雄浑的海景油画与清新有力的水彩著称，是十九世纪美国本土艺术的代表人物。

Winslow Homer was an American Realist painter who began as an illustrator of the Civil War. Renowned for his powerful marine oils and fresh, vigorous watercolours, he is a defining figure of nineteenth-century American art.
