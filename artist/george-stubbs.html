乔治·斯塔布斯

George Stubbs

1724–1806 · 英国

82 件作品 / works

乔治·斯塔布斯，英国画家，以解剖般精准的马匹肖像闻名。他曾亲手解剖马体、出版《马的解剖》，代表作《惠斯尔杰基特》被誉为动物绘画的巅峰。

George Stubbs was an English painter renowned for his anatomically exact portraits of horses. Having dissected horses and published The Anatomy of the Horse, he created in Whistlejacket one of the summits of animal painting.
