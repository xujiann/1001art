于贝尔·罗贝尔，十八世纪法国最负盛名的废墟与建筑风景画家，人称「废墟的罗贝尔」。他将古罗马遗迹与想象的宫苑熔于笔下，营造出诗意而略带感伤的怀古幻境，晚年更参与筹建卢浮宫，是最早的博物馆策展人之一。

Hubert Robert was eighteenth-century France's foremost painter of ruins and architectural landscape, nicknamed 'Robert des Ruines.' Fusing Roman remains with imaginary gardens and palaces into poetic, wistfully evocative capricci, he also helped organize the new Louvre, becoming one of its earliest curators.
