董其昌

Dong Qichang

1555–1636 · 中国

55 件作品 / works

董其昌，晚明士大夫画家、书法家与理论家。他提出山水画「南北宗论」，深刻塑造了后世文人画的取向；其山水结构峻峭、笔墨秀润，是文人画的一代宗师。

Dong Qichang was a towering late-Ming scholar-official painter, calligrapher and theorist. His theory of 'Northern and Southern Schools' of landscape shaped the direction of later literati painting; his own austere, structural landscapes made him a defining master.
