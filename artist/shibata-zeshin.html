柴田是真

Shibata Zeshin

1807–1891 · 日本

52 件作品 / works

柴田是真（1807—1891），日本江户末至明治的漆艺家兼画家，生于江户。十一岁入莳绘师古满宽哉门下学漆工，后随铃木南岭、冈本丰彦习四条派绘画。他复兴失传已久的青海波涂技法，并创出在纸上直接以漆作画的“漆绘”。1873年作品在维也纳世博会获奖，1890年任帝室技艺员。

Shibata Zeshin (1807–1891) was a lacquer artist and painter active in Edo and Meiji Japan. Apprenticed at eleven to the maki-e master Koma Kansai, he later studied Shijō-school painting under Suzuki Nanrei and Okamoto Toyohiko. He revived the long-disused seigaiha lacquer finish and devised urushi-e, painting with lacquer directly on paper. In 1890 he was named an Imperial Household Artist.
