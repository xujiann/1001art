奇马·达·科内利亚诺

Cima da Conegliano

1459–1517 · 意大利

42 件作品 / works

奇马·达·科内利亚诺，威尼斯文艺复兴时期的重要画家。他承继乔瓦尼·贝利尼的传统，以宁静庄严的「圣母与圣徒对话」及背景中澄明温润的风景著称，画面充满恬静的虔敬之美。

Cima da Conegliano was a significant painter of the Venetian Renaissance. Following the tradition of Giovanni Bellini, he is admired for serene sacred conversations set against luminous, gentle landscapes, works imbued with a quiet and devout beauty.
