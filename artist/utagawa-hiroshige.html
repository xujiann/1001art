歌川广重

Utagawa Hiroshige

1797–1858 · 日本

8 件作品 / works

歌川广重,日本江户时代浮世绘大师,以风景版画闻名。代表作《东海道五十三次》《名所江户百景》以诗意的构图与微妙的季节、天候氛围著称;与北斋并称,并深刻影响了梵高等欧洲画家。

Utagawa Hiroshige was a master of Japanese ukiyo-e landscape prints of the Edo period, celebrated for The Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō and One Hundred Famous Views of Edo. His poetic compositions and subtle rendering of season and weather, alongside Hokusai's, deeply influenced European painters such as Van Gogh.
