格里特·道

Gerrit Dou

1613–1675 · 荷兰

58 件作品 / works

格里特·道，伦勃朗的首位弟子，莱顿「精细画派」的开创者。他以珍宝般精微的小幅画作闻名，常以拱窗为框构图，对烛光与质感的刻画细致入微，生前声名甚至一度超过其师。

Gerrit Dou, Rembrandt's first pupil, founded the Leiden school of fijnschilders ('fine painters'). Famous for jewel-like small panels—often framed within a stone niche—and his exquisite rendering of candlelight and texture, he was in his day even more celebrated than his master.
