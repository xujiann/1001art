Charles Le Brun

1619–1690 · 法国

44 件作品 / works

夏尔·勒布伦，路易十四时代法国艺术的绝对主宰。身为国王首席画师与皇家绘画雕塑学院的实际领袖，他主持哥白林制造厂并设计凡尔赛镜厅的天顶装饰，为法国确立了庄严宏大的古典主义官方风格。

Charles Le Brun was the absolute arbiter of French art under Louis XIV. As First Painter to the King and effective head of the Royal Academy, he directed the Gobelins manufactory and designed the ceiling decorations of the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles, forging France's grand, official classical style.
