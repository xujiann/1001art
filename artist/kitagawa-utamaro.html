喜多川歌麿

Kitagawa Utamaro

1754–1806 · 日本

4 件作品 / works

喜多川歌麿，浮世绘「美人画」的至高大师。他以半身特写捕捉女性的个性与神韵，构图优雅、线条精微，作品经由日本主义深刻影响了欧洲印象派。

Kitagawa Utamaro was the supreme master of bijin-ga, pictures of beautiful women. His refined half-length portraits captured individual feminine character and grace, and through Japonisme profoundly influenced European Impressionism.
