尤迪特·莱斯特

Judith Leyster

1609–1660 · 荷兰

42 件作品 / works

尤迪特·莱斯特，哈勒姆圣路加行会中罕见的女性画师。她身处弗兰斯·哈尔斯的圈子，以明快灵动的笔触描绘欢宴人物与烛光场景，代表作《求爱》细腻动人，是荷兰黄金时代杰出的女性画家。

Judith Leyster was one of the few women admitted to the Haarlem Guild of St Luke. Working within the circle of Frans Hals, she painted merry company and candlelit scenes with a lively, spontaneous touch, her 'The Proposal' among the finest works by a woman of the Dutch Golden Age.
